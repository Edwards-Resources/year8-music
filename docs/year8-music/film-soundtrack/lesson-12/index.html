Starting the Assessment Task

Lesson 12 of 25 · Suspense and the Brief

We are learning to plan a soundtrack for a muted film scene in groups.

This lesson launches your assessment task.

In groups of three or four, you will re-score a muted film scene with original dialogue, foley and music. It's worth 20% of your Year 8 Music mark, split between composing and listening. Today you watch the scene and plan.

Today's plan

  1. Form your group of three or four.
  2. Watch the muted scene together, all the way through, before planning anything.
  3. Watch it again and complete a spotting sheet, marking where dialogue, foley and music belong.
  4. Decide who is taking the lead on dialogue, who on foley, and who on the music - everyone contributes to all three, but one person keeps each on track.
ComponentWeighting
Composing - your original music10%
Listening - your understanding of film and game sound10%
Total20%

Adjustments available

A shorter or simplified scene is a valid submission. Speak to your teacher before you begin if you or your group need one.

Need a hand? Take on one clear role

If planning as a whole scene feels like too much, choose one role - dialogue, foley or music - and focus your spotting sheet entirely on that one layer.

Go further

Write full cue sheets for your scene, noting tempo, texture and motif ideas for every music cue before you start recording.

Encore
