Film Soundtrack

Term 4 · 25 dates · Scoring the Scene

This term you score picture instead of a song. You'll investigate how music, dialogue and sound effects work together in film and games, and finish by re-scoring a muted film scene in a small group.

The question for this term

How does music shape the way we experience a story on screen?

The dates

Every lesson of the term, in order. Go straight to the one you need.

A

Sound and Story

Lessons 1 to 5
  1. 1The Sound of StoryWe are learning to identify how music and sound work together in film and games.
  2. 2Music and EmotionWe are learning how music shapes the emotions an audience feels.
  3. 3Foley: Building Sound by HandWe are learning how foley artists create realistic sound effects.
  4. 4Music as a Story StarterWe are learning to use music as a stimulus for creative storytelling.
  5. 5Spotting: Where Does the Music Go?We are learning to plan where music and sound belong in a scene.
B

A History of Scoring

Lessons 6 to 10
  1. 6Silent Films, Live MusicWe are learning how music supported storytelling before recorded sound.
  2. 7Leitmotifs and the Golden AgeWe are learning how composers use leitmotifs to represent characters.
  3. 8Synths Take OverWe are learning how technology changed the sound of film scores.
  4. 98-Bit and the First Game MusicWe are learning about the unique requirements of early video game music.
  5. 10When Music Reacts to YouWe are learning how modern game music adapts to player action.
C

Suspense and the Brief

Lessons 11 to 15
  1. 11Building TensionWe are learning how composers use dissonance, silence and dynamics to build tension.
  2. 12Starting the Assessment TaskWe are learning to plan a soundtrack for a muted film scene in groups.
  3. 13Recording Dialogue and FoleyWe are learning to record and layer dialogue and foley for our scene.
  4. 14Composing the UnderscoreWe are learning to compose music that matches the mood of a scene.
  5. 15Syncing Sound to PictureWe are learning to synchronise dialogue, foley and music with the scene.
D

Finish and Submit

Lessons 16 to 20
  1. 16Refining with FeedbackWe are learning to refine our soundtrack through rehearsal and feedback.
  2. 17Finalise and SubmitWe are learning to complete and present our film soundtrack project.Assessment
  3. 18Showcase and PresentationWe are learning to present our project and explain our choices.
  4. 19Reflecting on the ProcessWe are learning to reflect on our creative process and final product.
  5. 20Yours vs TheirsWe are learning to compare our work with a professional film score.
E

Reflect and Extend

Lessons 21 to 25
  1. 21Deep Dive: A Feature ScoreWe are learning how music interacts with storytelling across a whole film.
  2. 22Iconic Game SoundtracksWe are learning how game music adapts to and represents different worlds.
  3. 23Rescore ItWe are learning to creatively reimagine a soundtrack.
  4. 24The Power of SilenceWe are learning about the role of silence in film.
  5. 25Wrap-Up: A Year of MusicWe are learning to reflect on our growth across Year 8 Music.

Film Soundtrack

Due Week 7

Working in a small group, re-score a muted film scene with dialogue, foley and original underscoring.

The task notification and submission are in Canvas.

Outcomes

  • MU4-PER-01 uses performance skills to demonstrate understanding of the elements of music and communicate musical ideas
  • MU4-COM-01 improvises, arranges or composes using the elements of music to create musical ideas
  • MU4-LIS-01 uses listening skills to describe music in relation to stylistic, cultural, historical or social contexts and the elements of music

Class material. Assessment tasks, submissions and marks stay in Canvas.
