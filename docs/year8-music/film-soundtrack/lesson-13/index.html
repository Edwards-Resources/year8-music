Recording Dialogue and Foley

Lesson 13 of 25 · Suspense and the Brief

We are learning to record and layer dialogue and foley for our scene.

Record your dialogue and foley

  1. Draft a short dialogue script that matches what's happening in your scene.
  2. Record it on iPad, in as quiet a space as you can find.
  3. Record your foley separately, close to the microphone, one sound at a time.
  4. Import everything into your GarageBand or iMovie project.
  5. Name every file clearly - what it's for, not just “Recording 1”.
  6. Save into your shared group project.

Quiet on set.

Background noise recorded now is very hard to remove later. A slightly imperfect line recorded in silence beats a perfect line recorded next to a noisy hallway.

Need a hand? One line, one sound

If your group is short on time, record one clear line of dialogue and one clear foley sound properly, rather than several done roughly. Quality over quantity is marked, not just how much you attempted.

Go further

Add effects - reverb, EQ - to your recorded dialogue to match the space the scene is set in, for example a large hall versus a small room.
