Yours vs Theirs

Lesson 20 of 25 · Finish and Submit

We are learning to compare our work with a professional film score.

Hear the professional version

Theme From Jurassic Park

Your task - Venn diagram

Draw a Venn diagram comparing your group's soundtrack to a professional film score. What do you share, and where do the resources differ?

Key word: Texture

How many layers of sound are heard at once, and how they interact. A professional score might use a full orchestra; your project might use four or five GarageBand layers doing the same job.

Need a hand? Compare two things only

Compare just the number of instruments, and how the music changes over time. That's enough for a real comparison.

Go further

Analyse the professional score's use of leitmotif, texture and orchestration, and explain what resources it has that your project doesn't.

Encore

Name one thing the professional score does that yours doesn't, and one thing yours does that theirs doesn't.
