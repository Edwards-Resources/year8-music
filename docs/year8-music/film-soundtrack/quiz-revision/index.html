What the quiz is.

Part B of your assessment task is a listening quiz on Canvas, worth 10 marks. It is done in the computer lab under test conditions, and your teacher plays every excerpt twice. Every excerpt is music you have already heard in class, and every question uses words from this unit.

Answering the written questions

  1. Name the music word first, then say what it is doing. "The tempo gets faster" earns more than "it gets intense".
  2. Give a reason. Say how the music makes you feel that way, or why the sound belongs in that group.
  3. Give an example from a film or game you heard in class when the question asks for one.
  4. One or two clear sentences is enough. Every written answer is marked out of 1, 1.5 or 2.

Words to use

WordWhat it means
DiegeticA sound the characters in the story can hear
Non-diegeticMusic or sound added for the audience only
FoleyEveryday sound effects recreated and added afterwards
TempoHow fast or slow the music is
DynamicsHow loud or soft the music is
Tone colourThe sound of the instruments and how they are played
TextureHow many layers of sound are playing at once
LeitmotifA recurring musical theme that represents a character, place, idea or emotion
SynthesiserAn electronic instrument that creates sound from electrical signals
LoopA short section of music that repeats over and over
Adaptive musicVideo game music that changes depending on what the player does, rather than playing the same way every time

Need a hand? Sentence starters

Sounds: "___ is ___ because ___." Mood: "The music feels ___ because the ___ makes it sound that way." Film and games: "In a film the music ___, but in a video game the music ___. For example, ___."
