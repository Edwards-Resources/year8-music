Your task - Storyboard a game level

In small groups, storyboard a short game level and plan how the music would change as the player's situation changes.

Key words

Adaptive music

Video game music that changes depending on player actions or the game environment, rather than playing the same way every time.

Need a hand? Pick three states

Choose just three game states - calm, danger, victory - and describe one thing that would change about the music between each one: louder, faster, more instruments, a key change.

Go further

Research middleware tools like FMOD or Wwise, which composers and programmers use to build adaptive soundtracks.

Encore

Is adaptive game music still “composing”, or something else? Explain your answer.
