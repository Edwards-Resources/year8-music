8-bit Music & Chiptune: A Brief History

ListenThe most famous loop in games

LearnThree channels, one chip.

Early game consoles could often only play two or three notes at once, on a dedicated sound chip with no room for a full recording. Composers wrote short, tightly looped melodies because that was the only music the hardware could produce - and because a player might hear the same 8 bars hundreds of times in one sitting.

Your taskCompose an 8-bar loop

Listen to the Super Mario Bros. Ground Theme (Koji Kondo, 1985). Count how many different sounds you can hear at once. Then compose an 8-bar loop inspired by early game music on a keyboard, or in GarageBand if you have it: simple, catchy, and built to repeat without getting old.

Key words

Soundtrack
The collection of all sound in a film or game - dialogue, effects and music - or the commercially released music from it.

Need a hand?Copy, then change one thing

Start from a simple 4-note pattern on a chip-style synth sound. Change the rhythm, or one note, until it’s recognisably yours.

Go further

Research how the limitations of early consoles - the NES, Game Boy, Sega Master System - shaped what composers had to invent.
