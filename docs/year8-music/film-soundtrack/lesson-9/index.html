8-bit Music & Chiptune: A Brief History

Three channels, one chip.

Early game consoles could often only play two or three notes at once, on a dedicated sound chip with no room for a full recording. Composers wrote short, tightly looped melodies because that was the only music the hardware could produce - and because a player might hear the same 8 bars hundreds of times in one sitting.

Key word: Soundtrack
