Lesson 4 of 25 · Sound and Story

We are learning to use music as a stimulus for creative storytelling.

Your task - Imagine the scene

Listen without any picture. Imagine a film or game scene that could use this music. Write two or three sentences describing what's happening.

Key word: Motif

A short musical idea or pattern that is repeated or developed. A motif can grow into a leitmotif once it's tied to one character or place.

Need a hand? Sentence starters

“The music makes me imagine...” “It feels like the moment when...” “I can picture a character who...”

Go further

Write an extended scene treatment - a short shot list plus notes on where the music enters, builds and cuts out.
