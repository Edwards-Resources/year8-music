Syncing Sound to Picture

Lesson 15 of 25 · Suspense and the Brief

We are learning to synchronise dialogue, foley and music with the scene.

Lock it to picture

  1. Import your scene and audio layers into iMovie.
  2. Drag each layer so it starts at the exact moment it belongs.
  3. Nudge in small steps until it lands precisely - a frame or two matters.
  4. Play back at full speed and check every layer together.
  5. Get feedback from another group on one thing that's out of sync.
  6. Save.

Swipe the table sideways to see the rest.

Sync checklistDone
Dialogue lands on the moment it's spoken
Footsteps and foley match the action on screen
Music fades in and out rather than cutting

Type straight into the boxes. Your answers stay on this device and are still here if you close the page.

Close enough is not synced.

A sound effect that lands even half a second late reads as a mistake to an audience, even one that can't say why. Small adjustments matter more here than almost anywhere else in the project.

Need a hand? One layer at a time

Sync only the dialogue first and check it fully before touching foley. Sync only the foley and check it before touching music. Trying to fix all three at once is where sync work goes wrong.

Go further

Use crossfades, volume automation and layered textures so the transitions between your cues feel deliberate rather than sudden.
