Deep Dive: A Feature Score

Lesson 21 of 25 · Reflect and Extend

We are learning how music interacts with storytelling across a whole film.

Video

A trailer built almost entirely on its score

One scene is a sample. A whole film is a system.

Everything you've studied this term - diegetic sound, leitmotif, spotting, dynamics - works together across an entire film, not just one excerpt. A feature score returns to its ideas, develops them, and times its silences across two hours, not two minutes.
