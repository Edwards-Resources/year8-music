Same toolkit, new job.

Term 3 you built a track from the elements of music. This term you use the same skills - rhythm, harmony, texture, dynamics - to serve a picture instead of a song. Nothing new to learn, a new job for what you already know.

Your task

Watch the trailer excerpts again. Log three sounds you hear and classify each one as diegetic, non-diegetic or foley.

Key words

Diegetic soundNon-diegetic soundFoley

Diegetic sound - something the characters in the story could hear too. Non-diegetic sound - music or sound added for the audience, that the characters cannot hear. Foley - everyday sound effects recreated and layered onto a scene afterwards.

Need a hand? Ask one question

For any sound you're not sure about, ask: could a character in the story actually hear this? Yes means diegetic. No means non-diegetic - unless it's a sound effect like footsteps or a door, which is foley.

Go further

Find a leitmotif - a short musical idea tied to one character or place - that returns more than once in a film you know well. Explain what it represents and how you can tell.

Encore

Name one sound from today that only the audience could hear, not the characters.
