The Power of Silence

Lesson 24 of 25 · Reflect and Extend

We are learning about the role of silence in film.

Video

A Quiet Place (2018) - Official Trailer - Paramount Pictures

Silence is a choice, not an absence.

A film built around near-total silence still has a soundtrack - it's just made almost entirely of the sounds a character can't afford to make. Removing music is as deliberate a decision as adding it.

Your task - Mute it

Choose a music cue from your own project. Mute it and watch the scene again. Compare the two versions.

Key word: Dynamics

The volume of music, from soft to loud. Silence is the most extreme dynamic available, and the one composers use most sparingly.

Need a hand? Just vote

Watch both versions as a class and vote on which felt stronger. Then say one word for why.

Go further

Debate: “Silence is more powerful than music.” Prepare one argument for each side before the class discussion.

Encore

Name one moment in your own project where silence might work better than music.
