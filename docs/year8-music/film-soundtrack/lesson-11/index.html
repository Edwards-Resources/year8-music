Building Tension

Lesson 11 of 25 · Suspense and the Brief

We are learning how composers use dissonance, silence and dynamics to build tension.

Fear, built from almost nothing

Main Theme | From the Soundtrack to "Jaws" by John Williams

Two notes is enough.

The Jaws theme is famously just two alternating notes, getting faster and louder. Tension in film music usually comes from restraint and repetition, not complexity - a simple idea, tightened.

Build a suspense loop

  1. Choose two close or dissonant notes on a keyboard or synth sound.
  2. Alternate them slowly, then speed up over 8 bars.
  3. Add a tremolo or sustained string sound underneath.
  4. Drop to complete silence for one full bar.
  5. Bring in one sudden, loud hit right after the silence.
  6. Save your loop.

Key word: Suspense

Musical techniques - dissonance, silence, sudden changes in dynamics - used deliberately to build tension in an audience.

Need a hand? Follow the checklist

High pitch, played softly, followed by one sudden loud sound. That is a complete, working suspense idea on its own.

Go further

Compose your suspense idea using a chromatic or whole-tone scale instead of a major or minor one, and describe what changes.
