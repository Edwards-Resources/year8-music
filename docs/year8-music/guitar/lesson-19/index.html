Rehearse and Quiz Under Time

Lesson 19 of 25 · Rehearse and Refine

We are learning to prepare for both the performance and listening components of the assessment.

Half and half.

Half of today is rehearsal, with your teacher checking in on run-throughs. The other half is a short practice quiz, modelled exactly on the real listening assessment: three questions, timed.

Your task

Rehearse your chosen piece with teacher check-ins for the first half of the lesson, then complete the three-question timed quiz. Reflect on one strength and one area to improve from each half.
