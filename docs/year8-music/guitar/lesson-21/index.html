Guitar Performance

Lesson 21 of 25 · Assessment and the Band Project

This lesson is an assessment task

We are learning to demonstrate our guitar performance skills.

This lesson is assessed.

You perform your chosen riff, chord progression or short song excerpt individually for your teacher and the class, marked against the rubric you have been using since Lesson 17.

What you are marked on

  1. Accuracy - correct notes or chords, correct rhythm
  2. Technique - hand position and clean, confident execution
  3. Fluency - the performance keeps going rather than restarting
  4. Presentation - tuning, counting in and acknowledging the audience

Audience.

Respectful listening is part of the lesson. No talking, no phones, and applaud everyone.

Adjustments available

A simplified riff or a single chord performed in a supportive small group are valid submissions. Speak to your teacher before the lesson rather than on the day.

Going further

Add dynamics or a second layer, such as a duet part, and be ready to explain the musical choice you made.
