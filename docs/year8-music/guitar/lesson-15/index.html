Progressions and Global Listening

Lesson 15 of 25 · Chords and World Instruments

We are learning to connect performance skills with global listening knowledge.

Rhythm, then melody, then swap.

In small groups, rehearse your chord progression while one person strums rhythm and another picks out a simple melody over the top. Swap roles partway through so everyone plays both.

Your task

Rehearse your progression in role pairs, then listen to a short excerpt of world music and describe its tone colour using vocabulary from Lesson 14. Discuss with your group: does guitar tone colour imitate or contrast with what you heard?
