Riff Circle

Lesson 5 of 25 · Strings, Riffs and Tone Colour

We are learning to perform confidently for our peers and extend our tone colour vocabulary.

Your turn in the circle.

Every riff you have learned this week gets its audience today. Performing for two or three classmates is real performance: an audience, a start, an end, and applause.

Two more tone colours

Oboe - a reedy, nasal woodwind

Trumpet - a bright, metallic brass sound

Your task

In a circle, each person performs their riff in turn. While you listen, build a class vocabulary wall: add a tone colour word for each riff and each listening excerpt you hear today.

Key words

BrightMellowPercussiveResonant

Bright - a clear, sharp tone colour, often high-pitched. Mellow - a smooth, soft tone colour, often warm and gentle. Percussive - a sharp, rhythmic sound, like drums or muted guitar. Resonant - a full, echoing sound that seems to ring out.

Need a hand? Perform to one person

If the circle feels big, ask to perform to one partner first. The riff does not get easier by avoiding an audience, but a smaller one is a real place to start.

Go further

Describe your own riff's tone colour using two of today's words, and explain what about how you played it created that sound.

Encore

Which tone colour word best describes your own riff?
