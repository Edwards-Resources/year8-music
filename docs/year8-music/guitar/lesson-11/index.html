Your First Open Chords

Lesson 11 of 25 · Chords and World Instruments

We are learning to play E minor and A minor open chords.

Video

E minor and A minor, the first two chords most guitarists ever learn. Watch the finger shapes before you watch the strumming.

Six strings, one shape.

A chord is a group of notes played together, usually three or more. An open chord uses at least one string played open, not fretted, which is what makes these first shapes so much easier than the ones that come later.

ChordFingers down
E minor2nd finger on the 5th string, 3rd finger on the 4th string, both at the 2nd fret
A minor1st finger on the 4th string, 2nd finger on the 3rd string, 3rd finger on the 2nd string, all at the 2nd fret

Your task

Set up E minor, strum all six strings and listen for any buzzing or muted notes. Fix your finger placement until it rings clean, then do the same for A minor. Finish by changing between the two chords, slowly.

Key words

ChordOpen Chord

Chord - a group of notes played together, usually three or more. Open chord - a chord that uses open strings, not fretted on every note.

Need a hand? One string at a time

Strum one string at a time through the chord shape and listen for buzz. Fix whichever string is unclear before you strum the whole chord together.

Go further

Add D major to your two minor chords and practise moving between all three, in any order, without stopping.
