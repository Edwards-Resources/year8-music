Bringing It Together

Lesson 16 of 25 · Rehearse and Refine

We are learning to strengthen our performance skills across riffs and chords.

Choose your path.

From Week 7 you can perform a riff, a chord progression, or a short song excerpt for your assessment. Today you consolidate whichever one you're leaning towards, with a rehearsal log to track what's actually improving.

A useful warm-up

  1. Run a single-string riff, slow to fast.
  2. Rotate through your chord progressions in pairs.
  3. Note in your log what you practised and what still needs work.
  4. Play along with a backing track to check your tempo.

Your task

Choose a riff or a chord progression to focus on today. Warm up, rehearse it against a backing track, and fill in your rehearsal log honestly.
