Technique and Tone Colour

Lesson 9 of 25 · Fluency and Orchestral Colour

We are learning how playing technique changes a guitar's tone colour.

The guitar isn't one sound.

Where you pluck a string and how hard you press change its tone colour more than the note itself does. Play near the bridge and the sound turns bright and thin; play over the sound hole and it turns warm and full.

Your task

Play your riff from Lesson 6 three ways: picked near the bridge, picked over the sound hole, and picked with heavy finger pressure. Describe what changes in each version using tone colour words.

Key word: Picking

Plucking individual strings with a pick or fingers, rather than strumming across several at once.

Need a hand? Loud versus soft is enough

If three positions is a lot to track, just compare loud and soft on the same string. Even that small change gives you two clearly different tone colours to describe.

Go further

Try picking with your fingernail versus the pad of your finger, and explain which orchestral instrument's tone colour each one reminds you of.
