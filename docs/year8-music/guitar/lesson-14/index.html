Lesson 14: Instruments of the World

Lesson 14 of 25, in Chords and World Instruments.

Learning intention

We are learning to identify world instruments and describe their timbre.

Success criteria

Tick them off as you go.

ListenFive instruments, five origins

LearnEvery culture, its own instruments.

Guitar and violin are not the only string instruments in the world, and drums are not the only percussion. Every culture has built instruments suited to its own music, and each one has a timbre as distinctive as any orchestral instrument’s.

Key words

Djembe
a West African hand drum with a wide range of timbres.
Gamelan
an Indonesian ensemble of tuned percussion instruments, like metallophones and gongs.

Need a hand?Two families, one ear

The djembe and balafon are both percussion, like timpani. The sitar and shamisen are both strings, like guitar and violin. Use the families you already know as a starting point.

Go further

Compare the guitar’s timbre to the sitar’s or the shamisen’s. What about how each is built or played explains the difference?

Exit ticket

Name one instrument you heard today, and where in the world it comes from.
