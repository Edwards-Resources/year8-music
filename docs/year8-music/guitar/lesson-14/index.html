Instruments of the World

Lesson 14 of 25 · Chords and World Instruments

We are learning to identify world instruments and describe their tone colour.

Five instruments, five origins

Djembe - a West African hand drum

Gamelan - an Indonesian tuned percussion ensemble

Sitar - a plucked string instrument from India

Shamisen - a plucked Japanese string instrument

Balafon - a West African wooden xylophone

Every culture, its own instruments.

Guitar and violin are not the only string instruments in the world, and drums are not the only percussion. Every culture has built instruments suited to its own music, and each one has a tone colour as distinctive as any orchestral instrument's.

Key words

DjembeGamelan

Djembe - a West African hand drum with a wide range of tone colours. Gamelan - an Indonesian ensemble of tuned percussion instruments, like metallophones and gongs.

Need a hand? Two families, one ear

The djembe and balafon are both percussion, like timpani. The sitar and shamisen are both strings, like guitar and violin. Use the families you already know as a starting point.

Go further

Compare the guitar's tone colour to the sitar's or the shamisen's. What about how each is built or played explains the difference?
