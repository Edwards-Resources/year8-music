Wrap-Up and Showcase

Lesson 25 of 25 · Assessment and the Band Project

We are learning to share our ensemble learning and reflect on the term.

From one string to a whole band.

In Lesson 3 you were learning a riff on a single string. Today you perform as part of a band. That's a term.

The showcase.

Each group performs their song for the class. This isn't formally assessed; it's a chance to share what you've built and hear what everyone else did. Give supportive feedback after every performance.

Your task

Perform your group's song in the showcase, then complete the reflection table and the glossary below.

Go further

Evaluate your own development critically. What is the ceiling on your current guitar skills, what would you need to learn to get past it, and what's an ambitious but real goal for next term?
