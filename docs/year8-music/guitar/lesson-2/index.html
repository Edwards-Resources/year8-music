String Names and Tone Colour

Lesson 2 of 25 · Strings, Riffs and Tone Colour

We are learning to name the six strings and describe their tone colour.

Eddie Ate Dynamite.

Six strings, six names, from the thickest string to the thinnest: E A D G B E. The mnemonic Eddie Ate Dynamite, Good Bye Eddie gives you the first letter of each one in order.

Your task

Pluck each open string in order, low to high, saying its name out loud. Then compare the low E and the high e: which one is darker and more resonant, and which one is brighter and thinner? Write one tone colour word for each.

Key words

String NamesTone Colour (Timbre)

String Names - the six open strings on a guitar, low to high: E A D G B E. Tone Colour (Timbre) - the unique quality of a sound that makes one instrument or voice sound different from another, even playing the same note.

Need a hand? Start with the outside two

Just learn the two outside strings first: thick E and thin E. Once those two are solid, the four in the middle sit between them in order.

Go further

Play the same string open and then fretted at the twelfth fret. Explain in your own words why the pitch changes but the string's name doesn't.

Encore

Which string is darker and more resonant, the low E or the high e?
