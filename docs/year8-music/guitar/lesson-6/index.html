Two Strings Now

Lesson 6 of 25 · Fluency and Orchestral Colour

We are learning to perform a riff that crosses two strings.

Video

Come As You Are, on two strings. Watch which string each note falls on before you watch the rhythm.

One more string changes everything.

A riff on two strings asks a new question: which string is this note on? Your fretting hand has to move between strings as well as along the neck, and that takes rehearsal your first riff didn't need.

Your task

Learn the riff slowly, checking which string every note falls on before you worry about speed. Rehearse in pairs: one plays, one calls out the string number just before each note change.

Need a hand? Isolate the string change

Find the exact spot where the riff moves from one string to the other. Practise just those two notes, back and forth, until the move is automatic before you play the whole riff.

Go further

Once the riff is solid, work out the fingering that lets you play it without your hand shifting position at all.
