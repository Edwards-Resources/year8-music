Starting the Band Project

Lesson 23 of 25 · Assessment and the Band Project

We are learning to begin collaborative rehearsal in small ensembles.

Three weeks, one song.

The assessment is done. For the rest of the term you work in a group of three to five, choose a class-approved song, and rehearse it as a band.

Getting started

  1. Form a group of three to five.
  2. Choose a song from the class-approved list.
  3. Assign roles: guitar, keyboard, percussion, vocals.
  4. Start rehearsing your first section together.

Your task

Form your group, choose your song and assign roles. Use the rest of the lesson to begin rehearsing, even if it's just the first four bars.

Need a hand? Take a supportive role

Percussion or a repeated drone note is a real contribution to a band, not a lesser one. Take the role you can do well rather than the loudest one.

Go further
