Riff Fluency

Lesson 7 of 25 · Fluency and Orchestral Colour

We are learning to strengthen accuracy and control in our riff playing.

One minute, on repeat.

Fluency means the riff holds together without you having to think about each note. The only way there is repetition with attention: playing the same four bars until they stop needing your attention.

The fluency drill

  1. Play the riff for one minute straight, gradually increasing speed.
  2. Record a short video clip of yourself playing it.
  3. Watch it back and name one specific thing that slipped.
  4. Rehearse only that one thing before you record again.

Your task

Run the fluency drill on your riff from Lesson 6. Record two clips, ten minutes apart, and compare them: is the second clip actually more accurate, or just more confident?

Need a hand? Half speed counts

A riff played at half speed with no mistakes is more fluent than the same riff at full speed with three mistakes. Slow down before you speed up.

Go further

Play your riff along with a backing track rather than a click, and notice what changes about keeping time when the beat isn't a machine.
