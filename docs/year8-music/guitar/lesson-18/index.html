Your task

Complete the mock quiz, mixing orchestral and world instruments from the last two weeks. Mark your own, then discuss any you got wrong as a class and add missing vocabulary to the wall.

Need a hand? Family first, then instrument

Start every excerpt by naming the family: string, woodwind, brass, percussion or none of the above. Narrowing it down that way makes the exact instrument far easier to name.

Go further

For one orchestral and one world instrument that share a family, explain what makes their tone colours different from each other.
