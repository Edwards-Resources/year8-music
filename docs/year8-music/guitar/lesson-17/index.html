Performance Skills

Lesson 17 of 25 · Rehearse and Refine

We are learning to prepare for performance through rehearsal and feedback.

Presentation is part of performance.

Walking to the front, tuning up, counting yourself in and acknowledging the audience are all part of a performance, not just the playing itself. Today you rehearse the whole package, not only the notes.

What a confident performance looks like

  1. Check your tuning before you start.
  2. Count yourself in out loud, so the audience knows the tempo before you play.
  3. Keep going if something goes wrong, rather than stopping to restart.
  4. Acknowledge the audience at the end.

Your task

Perform for a partner or small group, then give and receive feedback using the scaffold below. Specific feedback names a bar or a chord, not just 'good' or 'needs work'.

Need a hand? Perform to one person

If an audience is the hard part rather than the playing, ask to perform to your teacher alone first. Do the run-through either way; skipping it does not make the real thing easier.

Go further
