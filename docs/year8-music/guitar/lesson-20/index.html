Lesson 20 of 25 · Rehearse and Refine

We are learning to consolidate our learning before the Week 7 assessment.

One more week.

Today is a structured run-through, not new content. Perform what you've prepared, then run a speed round of instrument identification to finish your listening revision.

What your teacher is checking

  1. That your riff, progression or song excerpt is chosen, rehearsed and ready.
  2. That your strumming or picking stays steady even after a mistake.
  3. That you can name an instrument's family before you name the instrument.
  4. That your tone colour vocabulary is specific, not just 'nice' or 'good'.

Your task

Run through your performance piece once without stopping, then complete a speed round of instrument identification with a partner testing you.
