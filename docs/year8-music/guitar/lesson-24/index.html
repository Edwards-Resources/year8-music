Ensemble Rehearsal

Lesson 24 of 25 · Assessment and the Band Project

We are learning to rehearse collaboratively and explore tone colour in an ensemble.

Balance is a decision, not an accident.

A band sounds good when every part can be heard, not just when everyone plays their best. Today's rehearsal is about balance and tone colour choices as much as accuracy.

Questions to work through as a group

  1. Is any instrument drowning out the others?
  2. Could a section sound different if one instrument dropped out, or layered in?
  3. Is the tempo steady across the whole group, not just within each part?
  4. What's one tone colour choice your group has made on purpose?

Your task

Rehearse your song, working through the balance questions as a group. Make and record at least one deliberate decision about tone colour, such as layering parts in or out.

Need a hand? One decision is enough

You don't need to solve the whole song today. One clear decision, made and agreed on by the group, is a real result.

Go further

Try two contrasting textures in the same section, such as fingerpicking versus strumming, and decide as a group which serves the song better.
