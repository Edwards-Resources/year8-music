Guitar

Term 2 · 25 dates · Striking the Right Chord

This term you build practical guitar skills, from your first riff to your first open chords, alongside tone colour: the element that makes every instrument, orchestral or from anywhere in the world, sound like itself. It finishes with three weeks in a band, putting riffs, chords and listening together in a real ensemble.

The question for this term

How can riffs, chords and strumming patterns be combined to create music?

The dates

Every lesson of the term, in order. Go straight to the one you need.

A

Strings, Riffs and Tone Colour

Lessons 1 to 5
  1. 1Parts of the GuitarWe are learning to identify the parts of the guitar and hold it correctly.
  2. 2String Names and Tone ColourWe are learning to name the six strings and describe their tone colour.
  3. 3Your First RiffWe are learning to perform a single-string riff in time with a steady beat.
  4. 4Families of the OrchestraWe are learning to identify the four orchestral families by their tone colour.
  5. 5Riff CircleWe are learning to perform confidently for our peers and extend our tone colour vocabulary.
B

Fluency and Orchestral Colour

Lessons 6 to 10
  1. 6Two Strings NowWe are learning to perform a riff that crosses two strings.
  2. 7Riff FluencyWe are learning to strengthen accuracy and control in our riff playing.
  3. 8Listening in IsolationWe are learning to identify orchestral instruments by their tone colour alone.
  4. 9Technique and Tone ColourWe are learning how playing technique changes a guitar's tone colour.
  5. 10Mock Listening QuizWe are learning to prepare for the listening assessment.
C

Chords and World Instruments

Lessons 11 to 15
  1. 11Your First Open ChordsWe are learning to play E minor and A minor open chords.
  2. 12Strumming in 4/4We are learning to strum a steady pattern in 4/4 time.
  3. 13G and C, and Your First ProgressionWe are learning to play G and C chords and move between them fluently.
  4. 14Instruments of the WorldWe are learning to identify world instruments and describe their tone colour.
  5. 15Progressions and Global ListeningWe are learning to connect performance skills with global listening knowledge.
D

Rehearse and Refine

Lessons 16 to 20
  1. 16Bringing It TogetherWe are learning to strengthen our performance skills across riffs and chords.
  2. 17Performance SkillsWe are learning to prepare for performance through rehearsal and feedback.
  3. 18Tone Colour ReviewWe are learning to refine our listening skills for the assessment.
  4. 19Rehearse and Quiz Under TimeWe are learning to prepare for both the performance and listening components of the assessment.
  5. 20Final PreparationWe are learning to consolidate our learning before the Week 7 assessment.
E

Assessment and the Band Project

Lessons 21 to 25
  1. 21Guitar PerformanceWe are learning to demonstrate our guitar performance skills.Assessment
  2. 22Listening AssessmentWe are learning to demonstrate our listening skills and tone colour vocabulary.Assessment
  3. 23Starting the Band ProjectWe are learning to begin collaborative rehearsal in small ensembles.
  4. 24Ensemble RehearsalWe are learning to rehearse collaboratively and explore tone colour in an ensemble.
  5. 25Wrap-Up and ShowcaseWe are learning to share our ensemble learning and reflect on the term.

Guitar Performance and Listening

Due Week 7

A guitar performance for your teacher - a riff, a chord progression or a short song excerpt - followed by a written listening quiz identifying orchestral and world instruments by their tone colour.

The task notification and submission are in Canvas.

Outcomes

  • MU4-PER-01 uses performance skills to demonstrate understanding of the elements of music and communicate musical ideas
  • MU4-LIS-01 uses listening skills to describe music in relation to stylistic, cultural, historical or social contexts and the elements of music
  • MU4-COM-01 improvises, arranges or composes using the elements of music to create musical ideas

Class material. Assessment tasks, submissions and marks stay in Canvas.
