Practice Stations

Lesson 10 of 25 · Rhythm and the Left Hand

We are learning to consolidate our skills through practice.

Practice is a skill you can be bad at.

Playing a piece from start to finish over and over is close to the least useful thing you can do. Good practice finds the two bars that keep going wrong and works only on those.

The three stations

  1. Melody station: right hand only, accuracy and fingering.
  2. Rhythm station: clapping and reading, crotchets, quavers and rests.
  3. Left hand station: drones and repeated patterns, in time.

Your task

Ten minutes at each station. Before you start, write down the one thing you most need to fix. At the end, write down whether you fixed it.

Need a hand? Pick the smallest thing

Do not write down get better at keyboard. Write down bar three, left hand, keeps coming in late. A problem that small is a problem you can actually solve in ten minutes.
