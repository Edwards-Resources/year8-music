Own Choice Piece and Listening Quiz

Lesson 22 of 25 · Test and Beyond

This lesson is an assessment task

We are learning to demonstrate our individual skills and understanding.

This lesson is assessed.

Two parts. You perform the piece you chose, then you complete the listening and notation quiz. Both are marked.

What you are marked on

  1. Own choice piece - accuracy, fluency and expression
  2. Listening - identifying step, leap and repeat
  3. Notation - writing down short rhythms accurately

Audience.

Respectful listening is part of the lesson. No talking, no phones, and applaud everyone.

Adjustments available

An excerpt rather than a full piece, oral answers instead of written ones, and a simplified dictation are all available. Arrange it with your teacher beforehand.

Going further

Justify your interpretation. Explain why you played a phrase the way you did, and what you wanted the audience to hear in it.
