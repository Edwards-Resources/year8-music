The C Major Scale, Left Hand

Lesson 13 of 25 · Scales and Hands Together

We are learning to perform a C major scale with our left hand.

Video

One octave, each hand on its own. The left hand fingering is a mirror of the right, not a copy of it.

The mirror.

The left hand starts on finger 5 and works down to the thumb, then the third finger crosses over the top of it. Same idea as the right hand thumb tuck, running the other way. Expect it to feel wrong for about ten minutes.

Your task

Left hand, one octave, up and down. Say the finger numbers out loud as you play them. When you can do it without saying them, do it with your eyes off your hand.

Need a hand? Watch where 3 goes

The whole thing works if the third finger crosses over the thumb at the right moment. Practise just those two notes, thumb then third finger crossing, twenty times, before you play the scale again.

Go further

Play the left hand scale in contrary motion with the right: both thumbs starting on middle C and the hands moving in opposite directions.
