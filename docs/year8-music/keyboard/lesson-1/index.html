High, Low and In Between

Lesson 1 of 25 · Pitch and the Keyboard

We are learning to identify high and low sounds and describe how a melody moves.

Welcome to the keyboard room.

Everything this term happens at a keyboard. Before you play a note you need one idea: pitch. Pitch is how high or low a sound is, and every piece of music you have ever heard is built out of it.

What you hearWhat it is called
A sound that is high, like a whistleHigh pitch
A sound that is low, like a truck engineLow pitch
Moving to the note right next doorStep
Skipping over notes to a further oneLeap
Playing the same note againRepeat

Your task

Play any note in the middle of the keyboard. Now play one that is higher, one that is lower, then the same note twice. Show your partner a step, a leap and a repeat without saying which is which, and see if they can name it.

Key words

PitchStepLeapRepeat

Pitch - how high or low a sound is. Step - moving to the note right next door. Leap - skipping over notes to a further one. Repeat - playing the same note again.

How this room works.

Two people to a keyboard, headphones in, volume at a level you can still talk over. Nothing gets unplugged and nothing gets carried. If a key is not working, tell your teacher rather than fixing it yourself.

Need a hand? Use your hand

Hold your hand flat and move it up for higher, down for lower. Steps are small moves, leaps are big jumps, repeats stay still. Show the movement before you try to name it.

Go further

Play a five note pattern for your partner using only steps, then the same shape using only leaps. Talk about what changes in how it feels, not just in how it sounds.

Encore

Play one step and one leap for the person next to you, and say which is which.
