Listening and Notation Revision

Lesson 18 of 25 · Refine and Rehearse

We are learning to prepare for the listening and notation quiz.

What the quiz asks you to do.

Two things, both of which you have done all term. Hear a melody and say whether it moves by step, leap or repeat. Hear a rhythm and write it down. There is nothing new in it.

Contour, in one listen

  1. Is the last note higher or lower than the first?
  2. Does it move in small moves or big jumps?
  3. Does anything come back?
  4. Draw the line before you write the word.

Your task

Six contour examples and four rhythm dictations, run as a practice quiz. Mark your own, then work out which of the two halves needs your revision time.

Key word: Dictation

Writing down music you hear rather than music you read. Rhythmic dictation is the version you are doing: hear it, count it, notate it.

Need a hand? Answer out loud

You can answer the contour questions by pointing up, down or flat, and the rhythm questions by clapping them back before you write anything. Ask your teacher if you would rather work that way.

Go further

Take down a dictation that includes both pitch and rhythm, not just rhythm, written on the treble staff.

Encore

Step, leap or repeat: which one has the flattest contour?
