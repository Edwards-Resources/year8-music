Lesson 17: Morning Mood

Lesson 17 of 25, in Refine and Rehearse.

Learning intention

We are learning to refine our skills and choose our repertoire.

Success criteria

Tick them off as you go.

ListenThe piece, as Grieg wrote it

LearnSay it out loud.

How does it feel? Which elements make it feel like that? Answer as a class before you move on. The feel chart on the wall has words if you need them.

Watch

The keyboard version. Slower and smaller, same tune.

LearnThe other option.

Morning Mood is the second piece available in the skills test. It is more of a stretch than Ode to Joy and it rewards expression more, because the tune is one idea repeated at different heights rather than a new idea each bar.

Your task

Work the stations again, but this time choose your own order and spend the most time on your weakest skill. Then decide which piece you are taking into the skills test, and tell your teacher.

Key words

Expression
The detail that shapes mood and style: how loud, how smooth, how much you shape a phrase, rather than only playing the right notes.

Need a hand?Be honest in the table

The table is only useful if you tell the truth in it. Marking everything confident means you get no help, and the skills test will find out anyway.

Go further

Listen to two different orchestras play Morning Mood and describe what each one does differently with dynamics and tempo.

Exit ticket

Which piece are you taking into the skills test, and why that one?
