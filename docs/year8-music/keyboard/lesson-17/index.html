Trial Run

Lesson 17 of 25 · Refine and Rehearse

We are learning to prepare for our skills test.

A rehearsal, not the real thing.

Today you do the whole sequence in order, in front of one other person, marked against the same rubric your teacher will use. Nothing counts. That is the point: find out now what falls apart.

The sequence, in order

  1. Right hand melody.
  2. Left hand accompaniment.
  3. Melody and accompaniment together.
  4. C major scale, one octave, hands separately or together.
  5. Your own choice piece.

Your task

In pairs. One performs the full sequence, the other marks it against the rubric and writes down one specific thing to fix. Then swap. Specific means bar and hand, not it was good.

Peer feedback

Swipe the table sideways to see the rest.

Need a hand? Perform to one person

If the audience is the hard part rather than the playing, ask to perform to your teacher alone, or to face away from the room. Do the run through either way. Skipping it does not make the test easier.

Go further

Perform your sequence to a small group rather than one partner, and take three pieces of feedback rather than one.

Encore
