Keyboard

Term 1 · 25 dates · The Keys to Success

This term you start at the keyboard and learn to read music from scratch. Pitch, rhythm, notation and technique, building towards a Keyboard Skills Test where you perform a melody, an accompaniment, a scale, and a piece you choose yourself.

The question for this term

How do pitch and rhythm combine to create melody?

The dates

Every lesson of the term, in order. Go straight to the one you need.

A

Pitch and the Keyboard

Lessons 1 to 5
  1. 1High, Low and In BetweenWe are learning to identify high and low sounds and describe how a melody moves.
  2. 2Reading the Treble ClefWe are learning to read and play treble clef notes.
  3. 3The Map of the KeyboardWe are learning to navigate the keyboard.
  4. 4Your First MelodyWe are learning to perform a melody using correct fingering.
  5. 5Step, Leap, RepeatWe are learning to identify pitch movement in music.
B

Rhythm and the Left Hand

Lessons 6 to 10
  1. 6Beat and RhythmWe are learning to understand the difference between beat and rhythm.
  2. 7Writing Rhythm DownWe are learning to write and read rhythm notation.
  3. 8The Left Hand Joins InWe are learning to perform left hand accompaniments.
  4. 9Melody and AccompanimentWe are learning to perform melody and accompaniment together.
  5. 10Practice StationsWe are learning to consolidate our skills through practice.
C

Scales and Hands Together

Lessons 11 to 15
  1. 11Ode to JoyWe are learning to coordinate melody and accompaniment in a new piece.
  2. 12The C Major Scale, Right HandWe are learning to perform a C major scale with correct fingering.
  3. 13The C Major Scale, Left HandWe are learning to perform a C major scale with our left hand.
  4. 14Hands TogetherWe are learning to perform a C major scale hands together.
  5. 15Write a Four Bar RhythmWe are learning to compose and perform short rhythmic patterns.
D

Refine and Rehearse

Lessons 16 to 20
  1. 16Morning MoodWe are learning to refine our skills and choose our repertoire.
  2. 17Trial RunWe are learning to prepare for our skills test.
  3. 18Listening and Notation RevisionWe are learning to prepare for the listening and notation quiz.
  4. 19Your Own FocusWe are learning to improve our skills through practice and feedback.
  5. 20Dress RehearsalWe are learning to demonstrate readiness for the assessment.
E

Test and Beyond

Lessons 21 to 25
  1. 21Keyboard Skills TestWe are learning to demonstrate our keyboard skills.Assessment
  2. 22Own Choice Piece and Listening QuizWe are learning to demonstrate our individual skills and understanding.Assessment
  3. 23Keyboard ChallengesWe are learning to celebrate our skills through challenges.
  4. 24The Group JingleWe are learning to compose collaboratively.
  5. 25Wrap-Up and ReflectionWe are learning to reflect on our progress and set a goal for next term.

Keyboard Skills

Due Week 8

A performance for your teacher: a right hand melody, a left hand accompaniment, both hands together, a C major scale, and a short piece you choose yourself.

The task notification and submission are in Canvas.

Outcomes

  • MU4-PER-01 uses performance skills to demonstrate understanding of the elements of music and communicate musical ideas
  • MU4-LIS-01 uses listening skills to describe music in relation to stylistic, cultural, historical or social contexts and the elements of music
  • MU4-COM-01 improvises, arranges or composes using the elements of music to create musical ideas

Class material. Assessment tasks, submissions and marks stay in Canvas.
