Keyboard Skills Test

Lesson 21 of 25 · Test and Beyond

This lesson is an assessment task

We are learning to demonstrate our keyboard skills.

This lesson is assessed.

You perform the skills sequence individually for your teacher, marked against the rubric you have been using since Lesson 17.

What you are marked on

  1. Accuracy - right notes, right rhythm, steady tempo
  2. Technique - hand position, posture and consistent fingering
  3. Fluency - the performance keeps going rather than restarting
  4. Coordination - the two hands hold together

Audience.

Respectful listening is part of the lesson. No talking, no phones, and applaud everyone.

Adjustments available

A shortened sequence, hands separately rather than together, or a smaller range on the scale are all valid submissions. Speak to your teacher before the lesson rather than on the day.

Going further

Present the scale hands together over two octaves, or carry dynamics and phrasing across the whole sequence rather than only in your own choice piece.
