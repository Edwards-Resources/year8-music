Dress Rehearsal

Lesson 20 of 25 · Refine and Rehearse

We are learning to demonstrate readiness for the assessment.

Full run, no stopping.

Everything, in order, without going back to fix anything. In the test you get one go at each skill, so today you practise carrying on after a mistake rather than restarting.

What your teacher is checking

  1. That you know the order of the sequence without being prompted.
  2. That your own choice piece is chosen, approved and learned.
  3. That your scale has a fingering you can repeat, not one you invent each time.
  4. That a wrong note does not stop the performance.

Your task

Perform the full sequence under timed conditions. If something goes wrong, keep going. Afterwards, write down the one bar you would most like another week on.

Need a hand? Shorten the run

A shortened sequence is a valid submission. Talk to your teacher before the test about which skills you are presenting and in what form, so that it is agreed rather than decided on the day.

Go further

Perform your sequence for another class group, or record it and mark your own recording against the rubric as if it belonged to someone else.
