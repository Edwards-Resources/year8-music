Reading the Treble Clef

Lesson 2 of 25 · Pitch and the Keyboard

We are learning to read and play treble clef notes.

Why bother with notation?

You could learn every piece by copying your teacher. Notation means you do not have to. It is a system for writing sound down so that a musician who has never met you can play exactly what you wrote.

Your task

Fill in the table above, then find middle C on your keyboard. Play an echo game with your partner: they play a note, you name it, then swap over.

Key words

Treble clefStaffMiddle CLeger lines

Treble clef - the symbol at the start of the staff that fixes the higher notes in place. Staff - the five lines music is written on, also called the stave. Middle C - the note at the centre of the keyboard and of the staff system. Leger lines - short extra lines used for notes sitting above or below the staff.

Need a hand? Start from one note

You do not have to know all nine at once. Learn where middle C sits, then count up or down from it, one line and space at a time. Every other note is a short count away from something you already know.

Go further

Work out where these same notes appear in the bass clef, and why middle C needs a leger line in both clefs.

Encore

Name the four space notes of the treble clef, bottom to top.
