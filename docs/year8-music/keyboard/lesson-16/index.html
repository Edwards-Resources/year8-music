Morning Mood

Lesson 16 of 25 · Refine and Rehearse

We are learning to refine our skills and choose our repertoire.

The piece, as Grieg wrote it

Grieg, Morning Mood from Peer Gynt - Seattle Symphony

Video

The keyboard version. Slower and smaller, same tune.

The other option.

Morning Mood is the second piece available in the skills test. It is more of a stretch than Ode to Joy and it rewards expression more, because the tune is one idea repeated at different heights rather than a new idea each bar.

Your task

Work the stations again, but this time choose your own order and spend the most time on your weakest skill. Then decide which piece you are taking into the skills test, and tell your teacher.

Key word: Expression

The detail that shapes mood and style: how loud, how smooth, how much you shape a phrase, rather than only playing the right notes.
