Your Own Focus

Lesson 19 of 25 · Refine and Rehearse

We are learning to improve our skills through practice and feedback.

You pick.

One lesson, one skill, your choice. Your teacher is conferencing one to one today, so the work you do here gets direct feedback on the thing you actually need.

Choosing well

  1. Look at your self assessment table from Lesson 16.
  2. Take the row you marked needs work.
  3. Narrow it down to one bar, or one hand.
  4. Spend the whole lesson on that. Not on the whole piece.

Your task

Choose your one skill, write it down, and work on it for the lesson. When your teacher reaches you, play the thing you have been working on rather than the thing you already do well.

Need a hand? Ask for the drill

If you know what is wrong but not how to fix it, that is exactly the question to ask. There is a drill for almost everything: slow it down, isolate one hand, isolate two notes, repeat twenty times.

Go further

Rather than fixing a weakness, take a strength further. Add dynamics and phrasing to your own choice piece so that it is interpreted, not only correct.

Encore

What did you work on today, and is it better than it was?
