Beat and Rhythm

Lesson 6 of 25 · Rhythm and the Left Hand

We are learning to understand the difference between beat and rhythm.

Video

Beat and rhythm are not the same thing, and mixing them up makes everything after this harder.

The beat is the floor.

The beat is the steady pulse underneath, the thing you tap your foot to. It never changes. Rhythm is the pattern of long and short sounds you play on top of it. One is the floor, the other is what you do standing on it.

Your task

In pairs. One person taps a steady beat on the desk and does not stop for anything. The other claps a rhythm over the top using crotchets, quavers and rests. Swap after eight beats. The tapper is not allowed to follow the clapper.

Key words

BeatRhythmCrotchetQuaverRest

Beat - the steady pulse of the music. Rhythm - the pattern of long and short sounds and silences. Crotchet - one beat. Quaver - half a beat. Rest - silence, written down so that it is played on purpose.

Need a hand? Walk it

Walk on the spot for the beat. Say the rhythm out loud while you walk: ta for a crotchet, ti ti for two quavers, shh for a rest. Your feet keep the beat honest while your mouth handles the rhythm.

Go further

Clap a rhythm that includes a syncopation, where a note lands off the beat rather than on it. Show your partner exactly where the surprise is.

Encore

Beat or rhythm: which one never changes?
