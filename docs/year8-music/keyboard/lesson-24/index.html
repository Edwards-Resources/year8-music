The Group Jingle

Lesson 24 of 25 · Test and Beyond

We are learning to compose collaboratively.

Four bars, one group.

In groups, write a jingle. Four bars long, with a melody and an accompaniment, and memorable enough that another group can hum it back after hearing it once.

How to build it

  1. Agree the rhythm first. Clap it until everyone has it.
  2. Put pitches on that rhythm using notes from the C major scale.
  3. Add a left hand drone or an ostinato underneath.
  4. Rehearse it, then perform it. Every person in the group plays something.

Your task

Write, rehearse and perform your four bar jingle. Every member of the group has a part. The test of it is whether the class can hum it back afterwards.

Need a hand? One note is a part

A part can be a single note played on beat one of every bar. That is a real contribution to the texture and it counts. Take the job you can do well rather than the loudest one.

Go further

Extend the jingle to eight bars, with the second four answering the first, and notate it so another group could play it without ever hearing you.

Encore

Could the class hum your jingle back? What made it stick, or not?
