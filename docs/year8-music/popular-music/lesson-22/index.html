Your task - The Australian sound

Brainstorm what makes Australian popular music distinctive. Think about language, accent, subject matter, instruments, and the stories artists choose to tell.

Baker Boy raps in Yolŋu Matha as well as English. That is a deliberate choice about identity, made in a genre that came from somewhere else entirely. Miles Phillips wrote Eastern Rosellas when he was seven, about birds at his grandparents' place, recorded it at home with his dad on drums, and went in at number five on the ARIA chart - the youngest Australian ever to chart.

Need a hand? One artist, one feature

Choose the artist you like most and name a single thing that makes their music sound Australian. One clear idea is enough.

Go further

Australian artists work in genres that began overseas - hip hop, rock, EDM. Explain how they fuse those global forms with something local, and what that fusion achieves culturally.
