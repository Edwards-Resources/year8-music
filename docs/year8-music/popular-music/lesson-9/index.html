Combining Drums and Chords

Lesson 9 of 25 · Chords and Harmony

We are learning to combine rhythm and harmony in GarageBand.

Video

Recording more than one track and stacking them up

What lined up looks like

Swipe the diagram sideways to see all four bars.

Every chord change starts on beat 1 of a bar, on the same beat as a kick drum. If your chord region starts anywhere else, the two layers will drift apart as the loop repeats.

Lock the two layers together

  1. Play your drums and chords together. Do the chord changes land on beat 1 of each bar?
  2. If not, drag the chord region so it starts exactly on a bar line.
  3. Check the balance - can you hear both, or is one drowning the other? Adjust the volume sliders.
  4. Loop the 8 bars and listen three times through. It should feel steady, not rushed.
  5. Save.

Checkpoint.

By the end of today you should have two working layers. Lessons 11-14 add bass and a riff on top of these.

Need a hand? Use a ready-made drum track

If your drum loop is not working, ask for the teacher drum track and layer your chords over that instead. The skill being practised today is combining layers, not rebuilding the drums.

Go further

Add a second 8-bar section with a different progression, so your track has contrast. Or vary the harmony the second time through - same chords, different voicing or rhythm.

Encore

Does your loop feel steady when it repeats? What did you fix?
