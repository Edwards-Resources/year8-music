We are learning how groove shapes style in popular music.

Your task - Groove Comparison

Clap each groove as a class. Then on your worksheet, draw a simple icon for each groove, note the tempo, and describe the feel in your own words.

Key words

BeatGroove

Beat - the steady pulse of the music. It is what you tap your foot to. Groove - the overall rhythmic feel created by the interaction of drums, bass and other instruments. Two songs can share a beat but have completely different grooves.

Need a hand? Clap it before you write it

Count “1 - 2 - 3 - 4” out loud and clap only on 2 and 4. That is a backbeat. Now clap on every number. That is four-on-the-floor. You have just played the difference between rock and dance music. For “describe the feel”, pick one: driving, bouncy, relaxed, heavy, floaty, urgent.

Go further

Transcribe one groove into simple notation on a four-beat grid, showing kick, snare and hi-hat on separate lines. Then compare the same groove across three Australian songs - does the tempo or the hi-hat pattern change the feel more?

Encore

Which groove is most iconic in Australian music, and why?
