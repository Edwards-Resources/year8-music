The Role of Bass

Lesson 11 of 25 · Bass and Hooks

We are learning how the bass works in a pop rock band.

Bass in four pop rock songs

Jet - Are You Gonna Be My Girl

Tame Impala - The Less I Know The Better

Midnight Oil - Beds Are Burning

Silverchair - Israel's Son

Bass does two jobs at once.

It plays the lowest note of the harmony, so it tells your ear which chord you are on. And it locks to the kick drum, so it drives the rhythm. That is why a track feels empty without it.

Key word: Bass line

The low notes that support the harmony and rhythm of a song. Usually the lowest part you can hear, and usually the one you feel more than you notice.

Need a hand? Sentence starters

The bass is the low sound that holds the song up. Finish these: “In Jet, the bass …” “In Tame Impala, the bass …” “In Midnight Oil, the bass …” Useful words: deep, punchy, smooth, steady, moving, simple, loud.

Go further

All four are pop rock, so the differences are small and worth naming precisely. Compare how much the bass moves: one of these plays almost nothing but the root note of each chord, another has a bass line you could sing on its own. Explain what each choice does to the feel of the song.

Encore

Which of the four has the bass line you would most want to play, and what makes it good?
