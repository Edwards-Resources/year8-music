Lesson 19: Solo or Group Performance

Lesson 19 of 25, in Texture, FX and Assessment.

  • Assessment task

Learning intention

We are learning to perform music with accuracy and style.

Success criteria

Tick them off as you go.

LearnThis lesson is assessed.

You perform your chosen Australian popular song as a soloist or in a small ensemble. Marked against the rubric.

Step by stepWhat you are marked on

  1. Accuracy - right notes, right rhythm, steady tempo
  2. Stylistic awareness - does it sound like the genre it comes from?
  3. Expression - dynamics and phrasing, not just correct notes

LearnAudience.

Respectful listening is part of the lesson. No talking, no phones, and applaud everyone.

LearnAdjustments available

A shortened performance, or a simplified version of the song, is a valid submission. Annotated lyric and chord sheets are available. Speak to your teacher before the lesson if you need either.

LearnGoing further

Perform an extended arrangement, include live accompaniment, or bring in a backing track you have made yourself.
