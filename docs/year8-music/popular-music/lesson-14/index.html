Recording Your Riff

Lesson 14 of 25 · Bass and Hooks

We are learning to record a riff that gives identity to our composition.

Recording a riff - video

Record your riff

  1. Add a track and choose the sound you planned in Lesson 13.
  2. Record your riff over the top of drums, chords and bass.
  3. Quantise if the timing is loose.
  4. Copy the region so the riff repeats across the loop.
  5. Listen to all four layers together. Can you still hear the riff, or is it buried? Adjust the volume.
  6. Save.

You now have all four required layers

drums, chords, bass and riff. Lessons 16-18 are about shaping them into a finished track.

Need a hand? Record it one note at a time

You do not have to play it live. Draw the notes onto the grid one at a time, in the piano roll. The result sounds identical and you control exactly where each note lands.

Go further

Write a variation of your riff for the second half of the track - same shape, one or two notes changed, or the rhythm displaced. Repetition makes a hook memorable; variation stops it becoming boring.

Encore

Which of your four layers is the most interesting to listen to, and why?
