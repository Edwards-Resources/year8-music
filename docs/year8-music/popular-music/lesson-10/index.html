Performance Preparation

Lesson 10 of 25 · Chords and Harmony

We are learning to rehearse and refine our chosen Australian song.

Your song must be locked in today

It has to be by an Australian artist, and approved. If you have not chosen, do that first - rehearsal time is short and the performance is assessed in Lesson 19.

Solo or group - your choice.

You can perform on your own or in a small group. Both are worth the same marks. Pick whichever you will play your best in, and decide now, because it changes which song suits you.

Rehearsal plan

  1. Play through the first section once, all the way, without stopping.
  2. Find the hardest bar. Play just that bar, slowly, five times.
  3. Play the bar before it and the bar after it, joined up.
  4. Play the whole section again. It should be cleaner.
  5. Write down what you will practise before next lesson.

Need a hand? Chorus only

Learn the chorus and nothing else. A chorus performed accurately and confidently is worth far more than a whole song played badly. Ask for a lyric sheet with the chords marked above the words.

Go further

Prepare a full verse and chorus, and accompany yourself - sing while playing, or add a backing part. Think about how you will start and finish, not just the middle.
