Recording and Programming Drums

Lesson 4 of 25 · Introduction and Drums

We are learning to record a drum part in GarageBand.

Video

Programming a beat on the grid with the Beat Sequencer

Build an 8-bar drum loop

  1. Add a Software Instrument track and choose a Drum Kit.
  2. Decide which groove you are building - backbeat, four-on-the-floor, or trap. Write it down before you play.
  3. Record kick and snare only for 8 bars. Ignore hi-hats for now.
  4. Play it back. If it drifts, use quantise to snap the notes to the grid.
  5. Record the hi-hats on a second pass, over the top.
  6. Loop the region and check it still feels steady when it repeats.

This is assessment work.

The drum track you build today is the first layer of your Week 8 composition. Keep it.

Need a hand? Start simpler

Use a preset drum kit and program the beat by clicking notes onto the grid instead of playing it live. Kick on 1 and 3, snare on 2 and 4, and nothing else. That is a complete, correct rock groove. Remember which is which: kick is the low boom, snare is the sharp crack, hi-hat is the short metallic tick.

Go further

Add a drum fill every 4 bars so the loop has shape rather than just repeating. Then try the same groove on a different kit - acoustic against electronic - and describe what changes about the feel.

Encore

Which groove did you build, and did you program it or play it in?
