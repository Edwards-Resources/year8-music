Your task

Apply at least two effects to parts of your composition. Listen before and after each one so you can hear what it actually changed.

Less is more

Reverb on everything turns a track to mush. Pick one or two parts that need it and leave the rest dry.

Key word: Reverb

EQ - adjusting frequencies (bass, middle, treble) to shape sound. Reverb - an effect that makes a sound feel like it is in a space, for example a room, hall or stadium.

Need a hand? Use a preset

Ask for the preset effect chains. Pick one, apply it to a track, and listen to what changed. You still meet the criteria - the skill is hearing the difference, not building the chain from scratch.

Go further

Automate an effect so it changes over time - a filter sweep building into a chorus, or a reverb throw on the last word of a phrase. Automation is what makes a track sound produced rather than assembled.

Encore

Which two effects did you use, and on which tracks?
