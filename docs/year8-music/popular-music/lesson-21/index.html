Lesson 21: Showcase Listening Session

Lesson 21 of 25, in Showcase and Reflection.

Learning intention

We are learning to evaluate compositions using the concepts of music.

Success criteria

Tick them off as you go.

Your task

Everyone plays their track. For each one you hear, note how the composer used the concepts of music. Use the proper vocabulary - that is the skill being practised.

Fill in

Track byTextureTimbreDynamicsOne thing I liked

Type straight into the boxes. Your answers stay on this device.

LearnFeedback rule.

Everyone in this room made something from nothing. Comment on the music, not the person, and find something real to praise before you suggest a change.

Need a hand?Sentence starters

“I liked the use of …”    “The texture was thick or thin because …”    “The loudest part was …” Listen to one or two tracks closely rather than trying to cover everyone.

Go further

Lead the feedback discussion for a track. Identify specific production techniques the composer used - not just what you heard, but how you think they made it happen.

Exit ticket

Which track sounded most different from yours, and what did they do that you did not?
