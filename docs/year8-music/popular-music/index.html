Popular Music

Term 3 · 25 dates · Battle of the Bands

This term we explore how Australian popular music uses rhythm, harmony and production to create style and identity. You will build your own track in GarageBand, and perform an Australian song of your choice.

The question for this term

How do the concepts of music shape the sound and style of popular music?

The dates

Every lesson of the term, in order. Go straight to the one you need.

A

Introduction and Drums

Lessons 1 to 5
  1. 1Welcome to Popular MusicWe are learning to identify characteristics of Australian popular genres.
  2. 2Rhythm and GrooveWe are learning how groove shapes style in popular music.
  3. 3GarageBand BasicsWe are learning to set up a GarageBand project correctly.
  4. 4Recording and Programming DrumsWe are learning to record a drum part in GarageBand.
  5. 5Drums in GenresWe are learning to compare rhythmic features across genres.
B

Chords and Harmony

Lessons 6 to 10
  1. 6Popular Chord ProgressionsWe are learning how chords create harmony in popular songs.
  2. 7Recording Chords in GarageBandWe are learning to record a chord progression in GarageBand.
  3. 8Harmony in StylesWe are learning to identify how harmony changes across styles.
  4. 9Combining Drums and ChordsWe are learning to combine rhythm and harmony in GarageBand.
  5. 10Performance PreparationWe are learning to rehearse and refine our chosen Australian song.
C

Bass and Hooks

Lessons 11 to 15
  1. 11The Role of BassWe are learning how bass underpins harmony and rhythm.
  2. 12Recording Bass in GarageBandWe are learning to record a bass line that works with chords and drums.
  3. 13Hooks and RiffsWe are learning what makes a hook memorable.
  4. 14Recording Your RiffWe are learning to record a riff that gives identity to our composition.
  5. 15Performance Check-InWe are learning to rehearse and refine our performance for assessment.
D

Texture, FX and Assessment

Lessons 16 to 20
  1. 16Texture and LayeringWe are learning to use texture to build density in music.
  2. 17EffectsWe are learning to apply effects to shape sound in a composition.
  3. 18Structure and ArrangementWe are learning to organise songs into clear sections.
  4. 19Solo or Group PerformanceWe are learning to perform music with accuracy and style.Assessment
  5. 20GarageBand CompositionWe are learning to create and present an original composition in popular style.Assessment
E

Showcase and Reflection

Lessons 21 to 25
  1. 21Showcase Listening SessionWe are learning to evaluate compositions using the concepts of music.
  2. 22Reflecting on Australian Popular MusicWe are learning to reflect on the cultural identity expressed through Australian popular music.
  3. 23Comparative ListeningWe are learning to compare how the concepts of music shape genre.
  4. 24Extension WorkshopWe are learning to extend our skills through self-directed projects.
  5. 25Wrap-Up and ReflectionWe are learning to reflect on our growth and identify future goals in music.

Battle of the Bands

Due Week 8

A performance, solo or in a small group, and a GarageBand composition.

The task notification and submission are in Canvas.

Outcomes

  • MU4-PER-01 uses performance skills to demonstrate understanding of the elements of music and communicate musical ideas
  • MU4-COM-01 improvises, arranges or composes using the elements of music to create musical ideas
  • MU4-LIS-01 uses listening skills to describe music in relation to stylistic, cultural, historical or social contexts and the elements of music
