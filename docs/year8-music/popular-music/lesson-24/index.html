Need a hand? Remix a short section

Take one loop from your track and change the instrument sounds and the tempo. That is a remix, and it is a genuine creative decision.

Set your own brief and direction. Advanced production, genre fusion, or recording live players are all open. Decide what you want to have made by the end of the lesson, then make it.
