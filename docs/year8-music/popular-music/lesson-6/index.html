Your task

Play I-V-vi-IV on keyboard or guitar as a whole-class jam, then practise it on your own instrument. Four beats per chord, round and round.

Key word: Chord progression

A sequence of chords that provides the harmony of a song. The same progression can appear in completely different genres - what changes is the instruments, tempo and production around it.

Need a hand? Start with two chords

Play just C and G, four beats each, swapping back and forth. That is a real progression and plenty of songs use only two chords. On keyboard, C major is the three white notes C-E-G played together. G major is G-B-D. Find them, hold them, and count four.

Go further

Try ii-V-I, or substitute a chord in I-V-vi-IV for one not in the table. What happens to the mood? Then work out the progression of a song you like by ear.
