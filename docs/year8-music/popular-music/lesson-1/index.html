Baker Boy and Jessica Mauboy are First Nations artists. Listen for how each one uses music to say something about identity.

Your task

Choose the genre you connect with most. Write two sentences about what you notice in its instruments or production.

Key word: Genre

A category of music that shares common features - for example rock, pop or hip hop. Two songs in the same genre usually use similar instruments, grooves and production choices.

Need a hand? Word bank

Use these words: distorted, clean, programmed, acoustic, layered, punchy, smooth, heavy, bright, echoey, fast, slow. Sentence starter: “In this song I can hear … which makes it sound …”

Go further

Find an Australian artist who is not on this list. Identify their genre and describe two stylistic features that place them in it. Be ready to play a 30-second excerpt to the class and justify your choice.
