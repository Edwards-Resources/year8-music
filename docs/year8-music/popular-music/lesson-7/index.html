Recording Chords in GarageBand

Lesson 7 of 25 · Chords and Harmony

We are learning to record a chord progression in GarageBand.

Video

Recording chords with Smart Chords

Record your chord track

  1. Open your project. Your drum loop should already be there.
  2. Add a new Software Instrument track. Choose a piano, electric piano or synth pad.
  3. Set the loop to 8 bars so it matches your drums.
  4. Record I-V-vi-IV, four beats per chord. That fills 4 bars, so play it twice.
  5. Play it back with the drums. If the timing drifts, quantise.
  6. Save.

Need a hand? Use Smart Instruments

Switch the track to Smart Piano or Smart Guitar. Tap a chord strip and it plays the whole chord in rhythm for you, in time. You choose the chords and when they change; GarageBand handles the fingers.

Go further

Layer two chord sounds - a pad underneath and a piano on top - so the harmony has depth. Or try inversions, keeping the top note the same as chords change, so the progression sounds smoother.

Encore

Play your chords to the person next to you. Do they sound pop, rock or EDM?
