Performance Check-In

Lesson 15 of 25 · Bass and Hooks

We are learning to rehearse and refine our performance for assessment.

Your task

Perform 30 to 60 seconds of your chosen Australian song for the class. This is a rehearsal, not the assessment - the point is to find out what still needs work while there is time to fix it.

Audience job.

Listen properly and give one thing that worked and one thing to work on. Be specific - "the timing in the chorus was solid" is useful, "it was good" is not.

Need a hand? Play the chorus only

A simplified section, played accurately, is a real performance. Use your annotated lyric and chord sheet, and play the part you know best.

Go further

Perform an extended section, and add accompaniment or a backing part. Think about stagecraft too - how you start, how you finish, and what you do if you make a mistake mid-song.
