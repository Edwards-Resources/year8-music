Recording Bass in GarageBand

Lesson 12 of 25 · Bass and Hooks

We are learning to record a bass line that works with chords and drums.

Video

Adding bass to your project

Record your bass track

  1. Add a Software Instrument track and choose a bass sound.
  2. Work out your root notes. For I-V-vi-IV in C, that is C - G - A - F.
  3. Play one long note per chord to start. Four beats each.
  4. Play it back with the drums. Does the bass land with the kick? That is the target.
  5. Once it locks, try playing the root note on every beat instead of holding it.
  6. Save.

The bass note is the letter the chord is named after. That is the rule, and it works for every chord in the table.

Need a hand? Use Smart Bass

Switch to Smart Bass and turn on autoplay. Choose a pattern, then tap the chord as it changes. GarageBand plays a bass line that already fits.

Go further

Add syncopated fills - notes that land off the beat - or write a walking bass line that steps between chords instead of jumping. Both make the bass a part people listen to rather than just feel.
