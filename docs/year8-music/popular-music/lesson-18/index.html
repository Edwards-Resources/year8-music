Build your arrangement

  1. Turn on arrangement markers in GarageBand.
  2. Label your sections: Intro, Verse, Chorus, Bridge, Outro.
  3. Make at least two contrasting sections - they must differ in which layers play, not just repeat.
  4. Strip the intro back. Drums alone, or riff alone, is a strong opening.
  5. Play it start to finish. Does it go somewhere, or just repeat?
  6. Save.

Key words

Drop

Structure - the arrangement of sections in a piece of music, for example verse, chorus, bridge. Drop - a point in EDM or pop where the music builds up and then changes suddenly for dramatic effect.

Need a hand? Use an A-B structure

Two sections is all you need. A = quieter, fewer layers. B = everything playing. Then A again. That is a complete, correct structure.

Go further

Build a full verse-chorus-bridge-outro form. Analyse an Australian song you like, map its structure bar by bar, then replicate that shape with your own material.
