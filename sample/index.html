Rhythm and Groove

We are learning to

understand how groove shapes style in popular music.

The three grooves

  • Backbeat — rock. Kick on 1 and 3, snare on 2 and 4.
  • Four-on-the-floor — EDM and dance. Kick on every beat.
  • Trap — hip hop. Sparse kick, clap on 3, fast rolling hi-hats.
GrooveWhere the kick and snare landTypical genre
BackbeatKick on 1 and 3, snare on 2 and 4Rock, pub rock
Four-on-the-floorKick on every beat: 1, 2, 3, 4EDM, dance, disco
TrapSparse kick, snare or clap on 3, fast rolling hi-hatsHip hop

Key words

Beat — the steady pulse of the music. It is what you tap your foot to.
Groove — the overall rhythmic feel created by the interaction of drums, bass and other instruments. Two songs can share a beat but have completely different grooves.

Success criteria

Your task — Groove Comparison

Clap each groove as a class. Then on your worksheet, draw a simple icon for each groove, note the tempo, and describe the feel in your own words.

Need a hand?

Count “1 – 2 – 3 – 4” out loud and clap only on 2 and 4. That is a backbeat. Now clap on every number. That is four-on-the-floor. You have just played the difference between rock and dance music.

Before you leave

Which groove is most iconic in Australian music, and why?
