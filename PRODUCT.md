# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML generated by a dependency-free Python build script from per-lesson JSON data files, deployed to GitHub Pages.

Inherited unchanged from the year10-music-2026 site, whose `build.py` and `course → topics → weeks → lessons → blocks` schema were confirmed generic with no Year-10-specific code. The reasoning that chose it still holds: no lockfile, no install step, nothing that rots between sessions, and plain static output for Pages.

## Users

**Primary: the projected board.** The teacher shows the site on the classroom screen and teaches down it. This is the main way the site is seen, so the first requirement is legibility from the back of a Year 8 classroom, not from a desk.

**Secondary: a Year 8 student on their own device**, aged about 13 to 14, off sick or suspended, or at home catching up. They need to find the lesson the class was on and understand what they missed without asking. Some students have a device at school and will open it there too.

**Also teaching from it: a co-teacher**, who takes one period a week with one of the classes and teaches the next lesson in sequence. The sequence itself has to be unambiguous to a second teacher who was not in the previous lesson.

Several Year 8 classes use this one site, and they are never at the same lesson number at the same time.

**No class codes anywhere on the site.** Decided 5 August 2026: class codes change every year, so the site names only the year group. Nothing student-facing, and nothing in the data schema, may be keyed to a particular class.

## Product Purpose

A public reference library of the full Year 8 Music year, lesson by lesson, taught from on the board and returned to by students who were not there.

Success is a student who missed a week getting back in sequence on their own, and a lesson taught from the site without opening anything else.

## Positioning

The whole year, openly navigable, with no "you are here".

The Year 10 site's distinguishing feature is that it tracks the class's live position. This site deliberately does not, and that is the product decision, not an omission: the classes run the same sequence at different speeds, so any single highlighted position would be wrong for most of them. Free navigation across the complete sequence is what makes one site serve every Year 8 class, this year and the next.

## Operating Context

- NSW public school, NESA Stage 4 Music on the Music 7-10 (2024) syllabus. The registered program is the authority; the site presents it and must never contradict it.
- 10-week terms, fortnightly A/B timetable cycle, 60-minute periods.
- Four term units across the year: Term 1 Piano, Term 2 Guitar, Term 3 Popular Music (Battle of the Bands), Term 4 Film Soundtrack. Build order is Term 3, then Term 4, then Terms 1 and 2, so the site is complete for the 2027 Year 8 intake.
- **Every class works in GarageBand on iPads.** Confirmed 5 August 2026. Ableton was only ever used for the Make Music online activities, not as a production tool for a class. Existing on-disk material implying one class produces in Ableton is out of date.
- Canvas remains the system for assessment: task notifications, submissions, quizzes and marks. The site links to Canvas for those; it never duplicates them.
- Students reach the site by URL, including from a link in Canvas.
- Two printed A4 double-sided placemats already carry the practical work and are deliberately class-agnostic. The site must not contradict them.

## Capabilities and Constraints

**Must carry:** the full lesson sequence for each term unit, per-lesson learning intentions and success criteria, listening material, class activities, and vocabulary and definitions.

**Must never carry:** student names, student work, marks, Sentral or markbook data, or commercial audio and video files. Audio and video are embedded from their host (YouTube), never uploaded.

**Must not carry a live position marker.** Explicitly decided. Not a tracker, not a "current lesson" highlight, not a most-recent badge. Any of these would be wrong for two of the three classes and would need syncing with a second teacher.

**Hosting:** GitHub Pages, public repository under the `Edwards-Resources` organisation, publicly reachable with no login. The site carries a `noindex` tag so it does not appear in search results.

**Repository and URL carry no year.** Decided 5 August 2026: the repo is `year8-music`, not `year8-music-2026`, because this site is meant to serve each Year 8 intake rather than one cohort. This deliberately breaks the sibling convention set by `year10-music-2026`, whose live-position layer does tie it to one class in one year.

**Legibility at projector distance is a hard constraint, not a preference.** Body copy sized for a desk fails on the board. The same pages must also work on a phone at home, so the type system has to hold at both ends rather than being tuned for one.

**School identity:** no school name anywhere, in any form, including the repo name, org name and README. No school logo, letterhead or brand assets. Recorded product rule, carried over from the Year 10 site.

**Publishing policy, unresolved:** whether NSW Department of Education policy places conditions on staff publishing teaching material to a personal external host, and the Code of Conduct's assertion of Department copyright in material created in connection with employment. Checked 5 August 2026, no definitive ruling found. The author is content to proceed on the basis that the Department owning these resources is acceptable. Noindex and no school branding stand as precautions.

## Brand Commitments

- Australian English throughout.
- No em dashes in anything student-facing or teacher-facing.
- **Its own visual world, deliberately not the Year 10 site's.** The Year 10 site's Marker Zine world is an anti-reference here, not a starting point. Each year group is to have a distinct identity, chosen from concrete options he approves rather than inherited.
- **Distinctive and engaging.** Same bar as the Year 10 site: a fully functioning premium website that Year 8 students find worth looking at, not a plain school course page.
- The structural virtues hold inside whatever world is chosen: an unambiguous course to topic to lesson hierarchy, short scannable lesson pages mixing text with embedded media, generous type, and accessibility as a requirement.
- **Australian-artist focus** in the Term 3 listening material is a content commitment already made (Thelma Plum and Eastern Rosellas chosen; further choices open).

## Evidence on Hand

- Five Canvas lesson-block pages covering all 25 Term 3 lessons, at `Year 8 (2026)/Term 3 - Popular Music/`. Real lesson titles, learning intentions, success criteria, activities and exit tickets, plus a teacher master page that is not published and must not be ported.
- Two built placemats: Building Your Beat (Beat Sequencer reference and the six required parts of the AT3 composition) and Chords, Bass and Hooks (four progressions, the root-note rule, the hook test).
- Worksheets: GarageBand beat grid, groove comparison, notes and scales, plus the Lessons 3 to 4 GarageBand tutorial script.
- The Term 3 vocabulary quiz and its Canvas import package.
- The registered spine in `Year 8 (2026)/Program and Assessment/`: assessment schedule and the four term programs.
- Syllabus reference at `School Master/Syllabus Reference/Music 7-10 (2024)/`.
- Terms 1, 2 and 4 material exists only as the previous cohort's files in `Year 8 (2026)/Archive (pre-2026)/` and will need rebuilding, not porting.

**Absent, and not to be invented:** the Term 3 listening selections beyond those already chosen, and any claim about student performance or engagement.

## Product Principles

1. **The board is the first screen.** Every type and contrast decision is judged at projector distance before it is judged on a desk.
2. **Many classes, one sequence, no "here".** Nothing on the site may assume which lesson a reader's class reached, or which class they are in.
3. **Public means public.** Every addition is judged on whether it would be safe on a billboard, because functionally it is on one.
4. **Built once for every class and every year.** No decision that only works for Term 3 2026, and no content-specific styling the next unit cannot inherit.
5. **It has to survive the author not being in a session.** No dependency, build step or service that will break unattended.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Students in these classes have access provisions applied under their learning plans. The site must remain fully usable with browser zoom and text scaling, meet WCAG AA contrast at minimum, and never depend on colour alone to convey structure or status. Listening activities must not assume headphones or a particular device.
