# Design System: Year 8 Music

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Tour Tee"**

A term is a tour and every lesson is a date on the back of the shirt. The whole system is a screenprint: a black print field where the cover lettering lives, bone garment stock underneath where the reading happens, and five loud spot inks, one assigned to each leg of the tour. Type is heavy grotesque caps carrying a single overprint offset, fields are bounded by 4px black rules rather than shadows, and halftone dot gradients fade off the bottom of every black field. Strip every word out and it is still a merch back-print.

The system is built to be read twice at very different distances. The primary screen is a projector in a daylit classroom, so reading surfaces stay light, learning intentions are sized for the back of the room, and step-downs in hierarchy are carried by weight rather than by grey. The same page has to hold on a Year 8 student's phone at home, which is why the type scales with `clamp()` at every level, the one wide object on the page becomes a named scroll region rather than shrinking, and the sidebar changes weight rather than position when the column collapses.

Loudness is rationed, and it is counted. Colour arrives as a whole-page decision, once per lesson; underneath it, exactly one block per lesson is printed loud and everything else takes the quiet weight. The Year 10 site's Marker Zine world is a confirmed anti-reference, as is the `sample/` directory's Rehearsal Marks world, which was built and rejected. Neither is a starting point for anything here.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Black print fields for covers and mastheads, bone stock for all reading
- Five spot inks, one per leg, never mixed on a page
- Black type on every spot ink, always
- Exactly one loud field per lesson, chosen by the build, never by hand
- A single overprint offset belonging to the plate, not the glyph
- Zero radius, zero blur: 4px black borders do the work shadows would
- Weight, not hue, encodes what a block is
- No position marker of any kind, anywhere

## Colors

Four flat spot inks plus a fifth, printed on a bone garment stock, with a near-black print colour that is never quite pure black and a reversed white that only ever appears on it.

### Primary
- **Print Black** (`#0B0B0D`): The ink of the press. Every border, every rule, every masthead field, all body type, and all type set on a spot ink. It is warmed off pure black by a hair so it reads as printed rather than as a screen default.
- **Reversed White** (`#FFFFFF`, written `#fff` in the stylesheet): Type knocked out of Print Black, and nothing else. The site bar, the skip link, the print-field logo, table column heads, the numbered step markers, the key-word chips, the "all dates" button, and the white pass of the plate offset on the lesson numeral. It is never a background: there is no white surface anywhere in the system.
- **Garment Stock** (`#EAEAE7`): The page. Every reading surface in the site is this bone, never white and never dark, because the projector needs a light ground in a lit room. Also the even rows of a table, and the `--ink` override on the outcomes panel.
- **Stock Highlight** (`#F7F7F5`): The lifted bone used inside bounded fields (panel interiors, table cells, track rows, prev/next cards, the breadcrumb strip) so a box reads as a separate piece of stock laid on the page.

### Secondary
The five legs. Exactly one is live per page, bound to `--ink` by an `.ink-*` class on the page's root element, and every child below inherits it. The letter mapping is fixed: leg A pink, B yellow, C cyan, D lime, E orange.

- **Tour Pink** (`#FF1E6F`): Leg A. Also the system's fixed hover and focus ink outside a lesson: the focus-visible outline, the poster cards on the home page, the prev/next hover, and the "all dates" button hover all use pink regardless of leg.
- **Tour Yellow** (`#FFE000`): Leg B, and the fixed fill of the term question box.
- **Tour Cyan** (`#00CFE8`): Leg C.
- **Tour Lime** (`#96F000`): Leg D.
- **Tour Orange** (`#FF6A00`): Leg E.

### Neutral
- **Soft Grey** (`#4E5058`): The secondary voice on a light ground: the footer, the section preamble under a heading, the swipe hint, the "ticks save on this device" note, the prev/next direction labels, the breadcrumb separator. Never used for anything a student must read off the board.
- **Print Field Soft** (`#D8D9DC`): The secondary voice on a black ground, one step down from Reversed White. The blurb paragraph under a logo.
- **Print Field Softer** (`#B9BABE`): The tertiary voice on a black ground. The lesson locator line ("Lesson 19 of 25 · Texture, FX and Assessment"). These two are a deliberate two-step reversed ramp, not two attempts at one grey: the blurb is a sentence to read, the locator is a line to glance at.
- **Hairline** (`#C4C5C2`): The 1px divider between tour dates, between numbered steps, between criteria rows, and between table rows. The quiet rule; the loud rule is always 3px or 4px in Print Black.

### Alpha derivatives
Three values in the stylesheet are not palette entries and must not be treated as new colours. They are existing tokens carrying an alpha channel, or a mask stop where only the alpha is read.
- `rgba(11,11,13,.28)`: Print Black at 28%, the scroll shade at each edge of the table region.
- `rgba(234,234,231,0)`: Garment Stock at zero alpha, the far end of the table region's fade. Written out in full rather than as `transparent`, because `transparent` interpolates through black and smears grey into the fade.
- `#000`: appears twice, only as the opaque stop of the halftone's `mask-image` gradient. In a mask only the alpha channel is read, so this value means "fully visible", not the colour black.

### Named Rules

**The Black-On-Ink Rule.** Type set on a spot ink is Print Black, without exception. White on the pink is 2.06:1 and fails; grey on any ink fails. This is simultaneously the print-accurate move and the accessibility floor, which is why it never needs to be argued about.

**The One Ink Per Page Rule.** A page picks up exactly one spot ink, from its leg, at the root element. Hue never encodes what a block is. If a lesson appears to need a second colour to distinguish two blocks, the answer is a different weight, not a different hue.

**The Light Ground Rule.** Black is for print fields and mastheads only. No reading surface, panel, or sidebar is ever set on a dark ground, because the primary screen is a projector in a daylit room. Reversed White is a type colour only; there is no white surface in the system.

**The No Colour-Alone Rule.** Leg colour is always doubled by a letter (A to E) and a lesson range; the assessment status is a bordered word in a bordered field, never a coloured dot. Nothing in the system is legible only through hue.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Archivo Black (self-hosted woff2, with Arial Black fallback)
**Body Font:** Atkinson Hyperlegible, 400/700/400-italic (self-hosted woff2, with Trebuchet MS fallback)

**Character:** A bootleg-merch grotesque doing all the shouting against a body face designed for low vision doing all the reading. The display face is only ever uppercase, tightly tracked, and set either at poster scale or as a small tracked label; the body face is never uppercase. Both are self-hosted, so the site has no network dependency and nothing that rots unattended.

### Hierarchy

The ramp is not one ratio. It is two ramps, one per face, and the split between them is the real hierarchy: the display face names things, the body face says things. Every size below is in the shipped stylesheet.

**Display face, poster tier** (uppercase, line-height 1.02 or tighter, negative tracking)
- **Display** (`clamp(2.5rem, 8.5vw, 5.6rem)`, `-0.035em`): the tour logo, one per page, on the black print field.
- **Numeral** (`clamp(2.6rem, 7vw, 4.4rem)`, line-height 0.9): the lesson number in the billing line.
- **Headline** (`clamp(1.8rem, 5vw, 3.4rem)`, `-0.03em`): the lesson title beside the numeral.
- **Headline small** (`clamp(1.5rem, 3.4vw, 2.2rem)`): the "The dates" section heading.
- **Poster** (2rem): a term name on a home card.
- **Date numeral** (1.5rem, tabular figures, right-aligned in a 2.1ch column): the lesson number in the tour list.
- **Leg letter** (1.35rem): the A to E capital inside its 40px ink square.
- **Title** (`clamp(1rem, 2.1vw, 1.3rem)`): a leg name.
- **Block title** (1.05rem): a block heading inside a lesson, and the assessment "when" line.

**Display face, label tier** (uppercase, positive tracking)
- **Brand** (1rem, `0.12em`): the site name in the bar.
- **Count** (`clamp(.82rem, 1.7vw, 1.05rem)`, `0.14em`): the "Term 3 · 25 dates · Battle of the Bands" line.
- **Label large** (0.8rem, `0.12em`, rising to `0.13em` on the leg range): the "all dates" button and a leg's lesson range.
- **Label** (0.74rem, `0.14em`, `0.16em` on the encore and the term question): the stamp bar on every panel. The most-used label size in the system.
- **Code** (0.72rem, `0.04em`): syllabus outcome codes, tracked tighter than any other label because they are alphanumeric strings, not words.
- **Meta** (0.7rem, `0.15em`): the lesson locator line and the masthead assessment flag.
- **Table head** (0.68rem, `0.1em`): column headers reversed out of Print Black.
- **Direction** (0.66rem, `0.14em`): "Previous" and "Next".
- **Flag** (0.6rem, `0.12em`): the assessment chip on the tour list, the smallest type in the system.

**Body face**
- **Lead** (`clamp(1.15rem, 2.5vw, 1.5rem)`, 700, max 60ch): the learning intention. Body face at bold, not display face: it must read as a sentence from the back of the room, not as a poster.
- **Question** (`clamp(1.1rem, 2.4vw, 1.45rem)`, 700, max 58ch): the term's driving question.
- **Strong body** (1.15rem, 700): a lesson title in the tour list, and the encore sentence.
- **Blurb** (1.1rem, max 58ch): the paragraph under a logo on a print field.
- **Body** (1.0625rem, 1.6, max 70ch): all prose.
- **Board** (1rem, 1.35): the learning intention as it appears in the tour list, and a prev/next lesson title. Sized for the projector rather than the desk.
- **Table body** (0.95rem) and **Body small** (0.92rem, 1.45): table cells; outcome text and poster subtitles.
- **Small** (0.9rem): the footer and the key-word chips. The numbered step marker shares this size in the display face.
- **Caption** (0.86rem): the `.small` utility and the table swipe hint, both in Soft Grey.
- **Crumb** (0.82rem, 700): the breadcrumb trail.

### Named Rules

**The Plate Rule.** `--plate` is `clamp(2px, .35vw, 4px)` and belongs to the print field, not to any glyph. Every offset element on a black field, at any type size, shifts by `var(--plate) calc(var(--plate) * .85)`. It is an absolute length precisely because a real second pass is off by one physical distance across the whole sheet, not proportionally to each letter. Never express the offset in `em`, and never give one masthead a different offset from another.

**The Overprint-Is-The-Only-Distress Rule.** One offset, no blur, no texture on the letterform, no rough edges, no fake registration marks. Bootleg lettering goes illegible fast; the single clean offset is the whole distress budget for the display voice, and body copy carries none of it.

**The Board Sizing Rule.** The lesson intention on the term list is set at 1rem with the title at 1.15rem, and the step between them is carried by weight, not by grey. Anything a student must read off the projector stays in Print Black at full weight. Soft Grey is reserved for what only matters at desk distance.

**The Two-Ramp Rule.** A new size joins one of the two ramps or it does not exist. Display face means uppercase and tracked, from 0.6rem to 5.6rem, and it names things. Body face means sentence case and untracked, from 0.82rem to 1.5rem, and it says things. There is no display-face body copy and no body-face heading, with one deliberate exception: the learning intention and the term question, which are bold body face because they are sentences that have to survive the back of the room.

**The No Kicker Rule.** No eyebrows, no kickers, no floating mini-labels above a heading anywhere in the system. A label is either a stamped bar filling the top edge of its own field or black caps sitting over an ink keyline inside that field. It never floats free above the thing it names.

## Layout

A single centred measure, `min(1160px, 100% - 2.5rem)`, applied by one `.wrap` class. Black print fields bleed full width; their contents sit in the same wrap, so the masthead lettering and the reading column share a left edge down the whole page.

Both interior pages use the same two-column split: a `1fr / 310px` grid with a `2.4rem` gutter, main column left, aside right, aligned to the top. The aside carries the durable references (assessment and outcomes on the term page; success criteria and the way back on a lesson page), the main column carries the sequence. Both grid children set `min-width: 0` so a wide child can scroll inside its own region rather than widening the column; without that, the wide table drags the whole page sideways on a phone.

Vertical rhythm runs on a small set of repeated steps: `0.6rem` inside a label, `1.2rem` between stacked panels, `1.6rem` between lesson blocks, `2.4rem` between legs and across the grid gutter, `3.5rem` before the footer rule. Panel interiors are `0.95rem 0.9rem`; stamped label bars are `0.55rem 0.8rem`.

Two breakpoints, both structural rather than cosmetic:
- **900px**: both two-column grids collapse to one column and the aside becomes a single-column stack with a `1rem` gap, falling below the main column so reference material follows the sequence. The sidebar's loud panel is demoted to the quiet treatment at the same time, because once it is no longer a separate column its filled bar would land directly under the encore and the lesson would end on two ink fields in a row.
- **620px**: the tour date row drops to two columns with the status flag wrapping onto its own line under the title, the leg range disappears, prev/next stacks and left-aligns, and the table's swipe hint becomes visible because below this width the table always exceeds its column.

Motion is minimal and functional: a `.14s` background and padding shift on a tour date, a `.16s` translate on a home poster, both on `cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.3,1)`. All transitions and animations are disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.

### Named Rules

**The No Position Marker Rule.** Nothing in this system may indicate where a class has reached: no tracker, no current-lesson highlight, no progress bar, no most-recent badge, no "you are here" in the breadcrumb beyond the page's own identity. Several classes run the same sequence at different speeds, so any marker is wrong for most of them. There is no position logic in the build and none may be added. This is the single most important rule in the project and it outranks every visual consideration in this file.

**The Straight-Down Rule.** The term reads as one continuous list from lesson 1 to lesson 25, broken only by leg headings. No card grid, no week accordion, no collapsed sections, no hero card before the list starts. The first viewport is the black print field and then the dates.

**The Demote-Don't-Move Rule.** When the two-column grid collapses, elements change weight rather than order or identity. The sidebar keeps its content and its place in the reading order and drops from the loud treatment to the quiet one. Nothing is hidden at a narrow width except the leg range, which is already restated by the list it heads.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat by construction. There is no ambient shadow vocabulary, no blur, and no tonal elevation ramp. Depth comes from the press: a 4px Print Black border defines a field, a 3px or 4px black top rule opens a quiet one, a 1px hairline separates rows, and the two tones of stock (`#EAEAE7` page, `#F7F7F5` field interior) say which sheet you are on.

Two shadows exist, and both are the overprint, not a light source.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **The plate offset** (`text-shadow: var(--plate) calc(var(--plate) * .85) 0 var(--ink)`): The second colour pass on every masthead. Zero blur, always. On the lesson numeral the direction of the passes inverts (ink glyph, Reversed White offset) so the numeral and the title misregister together.
- **The lifted poster** (`box-shadow: 6px 6px 0 var(--print)` with `transform: translate(-3px, -3px)`): Home page poster cards on hover only. Hard-edged, no blur, in Print Black. It is the same misregistration device at object scale, which is the only reason a hard offset shadow is admissible in this world; a soft or coloured drop shadow is not.
- **The scroll shade** (paired radial gradients at the left and right edges of `.tablewrap`, Print Black at 28%): Not elevation. A scroll affordance that rides the scroll via `background-attachment: local`, so the shaded edge disappears when you reach the end and the table stops looking clipped.

### Named Rules

**The Border-Not-Shadow Rule.** Weight and separation are always a black border or rule, never a blur. If a surface needs to feel raised, it gets a 4px border and the lifted stock, not a shadow. The only hard offset in the system is the print plate, and it is never soft, never coloured other than in the page ink or Reversed White, and never applied to a reading surface.

## Shapes

Zero radius everywhere. No element in the system has a rounded corner, a pill, or a circle. Every field is a rectangle cut square, which is what a squeegee gives you.

The border scale is a hierarchy in its own right, and it is how the eye reads importance:
- **4px Print Black**: a bounded field. The loud panel, the encore, the term question box, prev/next cards, the "all dates" button, home posters, the footer top rule, the underline beneath a leg heading.
- **3px Print Black**: the top rule that opens a quiet panel, the same rule applied to a demoted sidebar below 900px, and the table wrapper border.
- **2px Print Black**: small object borders. The status flag, the track-list rows, the tick square, the breadcrumb underline. Also the ink keyline under a quiet panel's label.
- **1px Hairline**: row division inside a run of like things.

Two recurring silhouettes: the **stamp bar**, a filled label strip that spans the full width of the top of its field and is separated from the interior by a 4px black rule; and the **solid ink field**, a bordered rectangle filled edge to edge with the page ink and set in black type. The leg letter is a 40px square of ink with a black display capital centred in it, never a circle.

## Components

### The Tour Date (signature)
The row that carries the whole product. A three-column grid link (`auto 1fr auto`): the lesson numeral in the display face at 1.5rem with tabular figures and right-aligned to a 2.1ch column, the title and intention stacked in the middle, the assessment flag right. Divided from its neighbour by a 1px hairline, never boxed.
- **Hover / Focus:** the row fills with the leg's ink, black type throughout, and gains `0.7rem` of left padding so the row physically steps out of the list. `.14s`, easing `cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.3,1)`.
- **Mobile (<620px):** two columns; the flag drops to the second column on its own line.
- Never carries a state beyond hover and focus. No visited styling, no completion styling, no highlight.

### Panels: the three weights
One ink per lesson, three weights of treatment, and the loud one is allocated by the build rather than by the block type. This is the core content grammar and Term 4 must inherit it unchanged.
- **Weight one, loud** (`.panel`): a 4px black bordered box on lifted stock, with a stamp bar filled in the page ink and black label caps. Exactly one per lesson, plus the two fixed sidebars (assessment on the term page, success criteria on a lesson).
- **Weight two, quiet** (`.panel.quiet`): no box. A 3px black top rule, the label in black caps over a 2px ink keyline, interior flush to the column. Every other titled block: key words, support, extension, listening, the unfilled-media notice, and titled prose and lists. Same ink, less shouting, so a lesson reads as a run of unequal things rather than six identical stamped cards.

**The `explain` block, added 16 August 2026, takes the quiet weight.** It is the long read: the block a student who missed the lesson can teach themselves from, at roughly 250 words against a site average of 210. It does not take the ink, because the task is still the thing to act on, and it introduces no second hue, no new frame and no new size. What separates it from every other quiet block is internal: it opens with a **call**, one sentence set at the learning intention's size (`clamp(1.15rem, 2.5vw, 1.5rem)`, body face, 700, `text-wrap: balance`), and the paragraphs follow at Body. That is the system's two-distance split done inside one block. The back of a daylit room reads the call off the board; the student at home reads the rest. It is the documented body-face exception applied to a third case, not a new one: a sentence that has to survive the back of the room.

Its label is `Liner notes`, fixed, never taken from the data. On a tour tee the liner notes are the written part you read at home to understand what you heard, which is precisely this block's job, and it follows the precedent set by `Encore` that a label here may be the world's own word rather than a functional one.

**An item in `paras` is either a paragraph or a list of strings**, the same either-shape the `listen` block's tracks already use, and a list renders as the same bulleted `ul` that support and extension blocks use. This exists because the first explain block written set five named controls as prose, and rendering it showed the five words a student has to write in their book buried mid-sentence. A set is bulleted; keeping both shapes in one field means the data decides where in the read the set falls.

`explain` is deliberately **not** eligible for the loud field. Adding a new type to `loud_block()`'s search order would re-allocate the ink on lessons that already have it, so the order stays activity, list, titled prose. One consequence worth knowing: on a lesson with no task block the loud field can land on a framing paragraph while the real teaching sits quiet below it, which is what Guitar Lesson 8 now does.

**Placed first in a lesson, an explain block's call sits directly under the learning intention at the same size** (Popular Music Lesson 6). Two Lead-size sentences in one screen reads as intention then claim rather than as competition, and the black rule and the stamp separate them, but prefer to place the block after whatever the lesson asks students to listen to first.

**The unfilled-media notice takes the quiet weight whichever block it arrives in.** An empty slot must never shout louder than the lesson around it, and it must never take the loud field: `loud_block()` skips placeholders, so a titled one cannot print "Not added yet" as the loudest thing on the page. Labelled `Listening` for a listen block and `Video` for a prose one, because the label has to say which kind of thing is missing.
- **Weight three, the way out** (`.encore`): a 4px bordered rectangle filled solid with the page ink, black type, closing the lesson. At most one per lesson, and only where the source data carries an exit block.

**Which block goes loud** is decided in the build, in a fixed order: the task block if the lesson has one, else the first list, else the first titled paragraph. Ordinary lessons put the loud weight on the task, because that is what the student acts on; assessment lessons carry no task block, so the marking criteria take it. Without that fallback an assessment lesson prints with no ink at all and ends up the quietest page on a site about printing.

The outcomes panel is the single documented override: it sets `--ink: var(--stock)`, so its stamp bar is bone rather than a spot ink. It is reference material, not part of the tour.

**A definition block naming one word puts that word in its own stamp bar** (`Key word: Tone colour`) and drops the chip, which would otherwise print the same word twice in a row. Several related words keep the plural `Key words` label and the chips. This exists because the Canvas extractor folded unrelated elements into single panels - timbre with dynamics, structure with drop, riff with hook - and a student reading one could not tell where the first definition stopped. One word, one box.

### Video well and clip grid
Added 6 August 2026, when the site got playable media for the first time.
- **`.video`**: a 16:9 well, 3px Print Black keyline, square, no radius, on a Print Black ground. This is the one black field that is not a print field: it is the letterbox behind the player, not a reading surface, so it does not breach the light-reading-surface rule. Capped at `46rem` on its own, because a 16:9 player run to the full column on a projector pushes the rest of the lesson off the board.
- **`.clips`**: a run of listening becomes a grid, `repeat(auto-fill, minmax(19rem, 1fr))`, not a stack. Seven players stacked at full column is seven screens of scrolling in a lesson. Each `.clip` takes `min-width: 0`, or the grid child holding a player refuses to shrink and pushes the page sideways.
- **Both sit inside the quiet panel**, so a filled slot and an empty one are the same shape and the lesson does not re-flow when a track is finally chosen. A player never takes the loud field; the task keeps it.
- Players are `loading="lazy"` and served from `youtube-nocookie.com`.
- A track with no embed yet keeps its place in the grid as a black well reading "Not added yet", rather than vanishing, so the run still reads as the complete set the lesson names.

### Status Flag
One status treatment on both surfaces, filled with the page ink. Display caps, black type, 2px black border, square, no radius. On the tour list it is the word `Assessment` at 0.6rem tracked `0.12em`, taking that leg's ink so each chip prints in its own colour down the page; on a lesson masthead it is the sentence "This lesson is an assessment task" at 0.7rem, taking that lesson's leg ink. Taking the ink rather than a fixed neutral keeps the marker loud on the one page whose job is finding your date, while still leaving it one rule and never a foreign plate.

### Home Poster
A 4px bordered card filled solid in pink with black display type at 2rem and a bold body subtitle with `text-wrap: balance`. On hover it translates `-3px, -3px` and drops a hard 6px black offset behind itself. Full height in an `auto-fill minmax(300px, 1fr)` grid with a `1.2rem` gap.

### Table
A named, focusable scroll region: `role="region"`, `tabindex="0"`, and an `aria-label` built from the block heading. The table keeps a `34rem` min-width, so on a phone it scrolls inside its 3px-bordered wrapper instead of squashing. Column heads are the display face reversed out of Print Black; cells alternate lifted bone and page bone. Above 620px the region simply scrolls; below it, a Soft Grey hint line above the wrapper says so in words rather than relying on a fading edge. The wrapper takes the same 3px pink focus outline as every other focusable thing.

### Table, worksheet variant
A table that arrives from `data/` with any empty cell is a worksheet, and its blanks become text fields rather than dead space. The build decides this from the data alone: no flag, no schema change, and a fully-filled reference table (Lesson 1's listening survey) is untouched. The field is a keylined box on the same stock, never a browser-default control: 2px Print Black rule, no radius, no inner shadow, body face at Table body size, `2.75rem` min height so it clears a touch target, and the same 3px pink focus outline as everything else. It picks up the row's zebra stripe so the alternation still reads. Under the wrapper, a Soft Grey line states that answers stay on the device. Answers persist in `localStorage` keyed to the page path plus the block index plus the cell, so two tables in one lesson cannot overwrite each other, and writes are coalesced on a 250ms timer because typing fires per keystroke. Each field carries an `aria-label` built from its row's first filled cell and its column head, so it is identifiable out of context.

### Beat grid
An inline SVG for showing a relationship in time that prose cannot carry. Four bars, one column per beat, drum rows below and a chord band above, with bar lines running the full height through both because the alignment between them is the whole point. Two colours only: Print Black rules and hits on lifted bone, chord blocks in the leg ink. Type inside the drawing is sized in viewBox user units on the elements themselves and is deliberately **not** on either page ramp, because it is geometry that scales with the diagram. The grid is described in data (rows of hits, a chord per bar), so another lesson can draw a different groove without touching the renderer. It scrolls in its own named region with the same worded hint as a table, and its caption is Print Black body copy, not Soft Grey, because the caption is the teaching point rather than a UI hint.

### Success Criteria
A loud panel containing checkbox rows divided by hairlines, 22px native checkboxes with `accent-color` set to Print Black. Ticks persist in `localStorage` keyed to the page path and never leave the device, which is stated on the panel in Caption. Below 900px it takes the quiet treatment along with the rest of the sidebar.

### Prev / Next
Two equal 4px-bordered cards on lifted stock, direction label in Soft Grey display caps at 0.66rem above the lesson title. On hover or focus the card fills pink and the label flips to black. Stacks and left-aligns below 620px.

### Focus
A single global treatment: `3px solid` Tour Pink with `3px` offset, on every focusable element including the table region. The skip link parks off-screen and lands as a black field with Reversed White type at top left on focus.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** set the leg ink once, on the page's root element, via `.ink-pink` / `.ink-yellow` / `.ink-cyan` / `.ink-lime` / `.ink-orange`, and let every child inherit `var(--ink)`.
- **Do** keep the leg mapping fixed as A pink, B yellow, C cyan, D lime, E orange in every future term.
- **Do** set black type on every spot ink, everywhere, with no exception.
- **Do** give every lesson exactly one loud field, chosen in the build by the documented order (the task block, else the first list, else the first titled paragraph), so a lesson with no task still prints.
- **Do** print one content shape one way. A block type takes the same treatment in lesson 2 as it does in lesson 19.
- **Do** let the status flag take the page ink on both surfaces, and keep it one shared rule.
- **Do** express the overprint as `var(--plate) calc(var(--plate) * .85)` with zero blur on every masthead element, at any type size.
- **Do** keep every reading surface light, and reserve Print Black grounds for print fields and the site bar.
- **Do** demote weight rather than reorder or hide content when a column collapses.
- **Do** give any element that scrolls a name, `tabindex="0"`, and a visible worded hint at the width where it will actually clip.
- **Do** set `min-width: 0` on any grid child that can contain a wide object.
- **Do** double every colour signal with a letter, a word, or a range.
- **Do** self-host any new face as woff2 in `assets/fonts/`, so the site keeps working with no network and no dependency.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add a live position marker of any kind, ever: no tracker, no current-lesson highlight, no progress bar, no most-recent badge. Several classes run the same sequence at different speeds. There is no position logic in the build, and adding one breaks the product.
- **Don't** use hue to encode block type. Two blocks in one lesson are never two different inks.
- **Don't** print a second loud field in a lesson, and don't leave a lesson with none.
- **Don't** stack two filled ink fields directly on top of each other at any width; that is what the 900px sidebar demotion exists to prevent.
- **Don't** set Reversed White or grey type on a spot ink, and don't set body copy in Soft Grey when it has to be read off the board.
- **Don't** introduce a white surface. Reversed White is a type colour on Print Black only.
- **Don't** add a kicker, eyebrow, or free-floating label above a heading. A label is a stamp bar or keylined caps inside its own field.
- **Don't** round a corner, blur a shadow, or add a soft or coloured drop shadow. The only hard offset permitted is the print plate at object scale, in Print Black.
- **Don't** express the plate offset in `em` or vary it per element; it belongs to the sheet, not the glyph.
- **Don't** introduce a dark reading surface, a card grid, a week accordion, or a hero card before the tour list.
- **Don't** add a sixth spot ink, a tint of an existing one, or a new grey. The two reversed greys and the one Soft Grey are the whole secondary-voice budget.
- **Don't** add a font size that is not on one of the two ramps above.
- **Don't** treat `sample/index.html`, `sample/y8-sample-standalone.html`, or the Year 10 Marker Zine site as a reference; both are rejected worlds.
- **Don't** add distress texture, registration marks, rough edges, or a second offset to the lettering. One clean offset is the entire distress budget.
